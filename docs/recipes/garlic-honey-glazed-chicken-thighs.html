Garlic-Honey Glazed Chicken Thighs

One-pan, weeknight-easy, restaurant-style lacquered chicken. Works with boneless breasts too (see note).

Serves 2–3Prep 10 minCook 20 min

🧂 Ingredients

Chicken

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs (~1¼ lbs total)
  • Kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter

Garlic-Honey Sauce

  • 4 cloves garlic, finely minced (or thinly sliced for milder flavor)
  • 2½ tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar
  • ½ tsp Dijon mustard (optional but adds depth)
  • Pinch red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1–2 tsp water, to loosen glaze if needed

To Finish

  • Fresh parsley or chives, finely chopped
  • Lemon wedge

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Pat thighs dry with paper towels, season both sides generously with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet (cast-iron preferred) over medium-high heat until shimmering. Lay chicken smooth-side down, do not move for 4–5 minutes until deep golden crust forms.
  3. Flip, reduce heat to medium, add butter, baste (arroser) with the foaming butter for 3–4 minutes. Remove when internal temp hits 170°F (77°C). Set aside.
  4. Lower heat to medium-low. Add garlic, sauté 20–30 seconds until just aromatic (don't brown). Stir in honey, soy sauce, vinegar, mustard, and chili flakes. Simmer 1–2 minutes until glossy and slightly thickened. Add a splash of water if too tight.
  5. Return thighs to the pan, toss or spoon glaze over to fully coat. Simmer 30–60 seconds so the sauce clings in a shiny lacquer.

Plating

Spoon extra glaze across the plate, place thighs on top, sprinkle herbs, squeeze of lemon. Serve with roasted or steamed broccoli, jasmine rice, or mashed potatoes.

Chicken Breast Version

Works the same way with 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (6–7 oz each) — pound to even thickness first, sear to 160°F (71°C) internal before resting.

💡 Chef's Notes

The soy + honey creates controlled caramelization — don't rush it. A touch of acid (vinegar or lemon) keeps it from being cloying.
