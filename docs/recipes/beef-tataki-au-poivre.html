Beef Tataki au Poivre

Fusion of French "au poivre" (peppercorn-crusted steak) and Japanese "tataki" (lightly seared beef with ponzu-style dressing).

Prep 15 minCook 8 minMarinate/Rest 1 hourServes 4

🧂 Ingredients

Beef

  • 1 lb (450g) beef tenderloin or sirloin, trimmed
  • 1 tbsp black peppercorns, coarsely crushed
  • 1 tbsp pink or green peppercorns, coarsely crushed (optional)
  • 1½ tsp sea salt
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil (grapeseed or avocado)
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, lightly smashed
  • 1 sprig thyme (optional)

Marinade/Dressing

  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar or yuzu juice
  • 1 tbsp mirin or dry sherry
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp sugar or honey
  • 1 small shallot, finely minced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Garnish

  • Thinly sliced green onions, toasted sesame seeds, microgreens or radish (optional)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Pat beef dry, season with sea salt. Crush peppercorns and press evenly over the beef to form a crust. Let sit at room temp 20 minutes.
  2. Heat a cast-iron pan over high heat with neutral oil. Sear beef each side 30–45 seconds, rotating for even browning, keeping the inside rare. Reduce heat slightly, add butter, garlic, thyme, baste for 30 seconds. Remove, rest 10 minutes, then wrap and refrigerate at least 1 hour to firm up.
  3. Whisk soy sauce, rice vinegar, mirin, Dijon, sugar, shallot, sesame oil until emulsified. Adjust for balance.
  4. Slice chilled beef very thinly across the grain. Arrange on a chilled plate, spoon dressing over, garnish with green onions, sesame seeds, microgreens.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Beef should be rare to medium-rare for authentic texture.
  • Freeze 20 minutes before slicing for ultra-thin cuts.
  • Serve cold or room temp with rice, daikon salad, or ponzu dip.

📊 Nutrition (per serving)

~310 cal, 30g protein, 18g fat, 4g carbs, 2g sugar, 720mg sodium.
