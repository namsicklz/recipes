Bang Bang Chicken Crispy Rice Paper Dumplings

Makes 8.

🧂 Ingredients

Bang Bang Sauce

  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Chinese black vinegar
  • 2 tsp honey (or sugar)
  • 2 tbsp chilli oil (with crisp)

Filling

  • 1½ cups shredded chicken
  • ½ cup purple cabbage
  • ½ cup green cabbage
  • 1 medium carrot
  • 2 spring onions
  • 3–4 tbsp bang bang sauce

To Wrap

  • Rice paper sheets (2 per dumpling)
  • Avocado slices
  • Cooked rice

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Whisk bang bang sauce ingredients until smooth. Adjust sweetness/heat as needed.
  2. Mix shredded chicken, cabbages, carrot, spring onions with 3–4 tbsp bang bang sauce — just enough to coat the slaw.
  3. Dip 2 rice paper sheets quickly in warm water. Place avocado slices on the rice paper, add a small tablespoon of cooked rice, then 2–3 tbsp chicken slaw. Fold and roll tightly like a spring roll.
  4. Pan fry in a little oil over medium heat until golden and blistered on all sides (2–3 min per side), or air fry at 200°C for 7–9 minutes, flipping halfway.
  5. Serve with leftover bang bang sauce for dipping, extra chilli oil if desired.
