Grilled Zucchini & Chickpea Wraps with Herbed Yogurt Sauce

Makes 4 wraps.

🧂 Ingredients

Wraps

  • 2 medium zucchinis, sliced lengthwise into thin strips
  • 1 can (15 oz / 400g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • ½ cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 1 cup baby spinach or arugula
  • 4 whole wheat or spinach tortillas

Herbed Yogurt Sauce

  • ½ cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh herbs (parsley, dill, mint, or cilantro)
  • Salt & pepper to taste

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Mix Greek yogurt, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and chopped herbs. Season with salt and pepper. Chill until ready to use.
  2. Preheat a grill pan or outdoor grill over medium-high heat. Brush zucchini strips with olive oil, season lightly with salt and pepper. Grill 2–3 minutes per side until tender and lightly charred. Set aside.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add chickpeas, smoked paprika, cumin, salt, pepper. Cook 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden and fragrant.
  4. Warm tortillas slightly. Spread a generous spoonful of herbed yogurt sauce in the center. Add spinach or arugula, grilled zucchini, chickpeas, and feta. Roll tightly into a wrap.
  5. Slice in half, serve warm or at room temperature. Optional: drizzle with extra yogurt sauce or a squeeze of lemon.
