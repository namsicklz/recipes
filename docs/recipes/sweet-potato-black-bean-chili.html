Sweet Potato & Black Bean Chili

Source: EatingWell

Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 1 medium-large sweet potato, peeled and diced
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 4 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp ground chipotle chile
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 2½ cups water
  • 2 (15-oz) cans black beans, rinsed
  • 1 (14-oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 4 tsp lime juice
  • ½ cup chopped fresh cilantro

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add sweet potato and onion; cook until onion softens, ~4 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, chili powder, cumin, chipotle, and salt; cook, stirring, ~30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Stir in water, bring to a simmer, add black beans, diced tomatoes, and lime juice. Simmer until sweet potato is tender, 10–12 minutes.
  4. Stir in cilantro, serve warm.

Nutrition (per serving)

323 cal, 13g protein, 8g fat, 55g carbs.
