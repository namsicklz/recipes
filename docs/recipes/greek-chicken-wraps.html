Greek Chicken Wraps

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 2 lb boneless chicken breast, halved lengthwise for even thickness
  • 3 tbsp full fat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp freshly grated garlic
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder

Greek Salad

  • 1 large red onion, finely diced
  • 1 large cucumber, finely diced
  • 1–2 tomatoes, deseeded and diced
  • ½ cup olives, diced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ cup feta cheese, optional

Tzatziki Sauce

  • 1 cup whole milk Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp freshly grated garlic
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • ½ cup cucumber, grated and squeezed dry

Assembly

  • 6 large tortilla wraps
  • 2 cups lettuce

Instructions

  1. Combine yogurt, mayonnaise, lemon juice, garlic, spices, salt, and olive oil. Coat chicken thoroughly. Cover, refrigerate 2–4 hours (overnight best).
  2. Grill or pan-sear chicken over medium-low heat 5–7 minutes per side until cooked through, then briefly increase heat to char. Rest 5 minutes, slice into strips.
  3. Combine Greek salad ingredients; season and toss (add feta if using).
  4. Mix tzatziki ingredients; chill until ready.
  5. Warm tortillas, layer with lettuce, chicken, Greek salad, tzatziki. Wrap tightly, serve immediately.
