Poulet Sauté à la Grand-Mère (French Grandmother's Chicken)

Comforting French country classic — chicken sautéed with bacon, pearl onions, and mushrooms in a rich wine sauce.

🧂 Ingredients (serves 4)

Chicken

  • 1 whole chicken (3½–4 lbs), cut into 8 pieces (or 4 bone-in thighs + 4 drumsticks)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil or butter
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tbsp flour (for light dredging)

Sauce

  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 4 oz (115g) slab bacon or lardons, diced
  • 8–10 pearl onions, peeled
  • 8 oz (225g) button mushrooms, halved
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup dry white wine
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 2–3 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 bay leaf

Finishing

  • 2 tbsp cold butter, diced
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • Optional: 1 tbsp brandy or cognac for deglazing

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Pat chicken dry, season with salt and pepper, dust lightly with flour. Brown on all sides in olive oil + butter over medium-high heat (4–5 min per side). Remove and set aside.
  2. In the same pan, cook diced bacon until just crisp. Add pearl onions, cook until caramelized (~5 min). Stir in mushrooms until golden. Add garlic and tomato paste; cook 1 minute.
  3. Deglaze with white wine (and brandy if using), scraping up browned bits. Stir in chicken stock, thyme, bay leaf. Return chicken to pan, cover, simmer 25–30 minutes until cooked through and tender.
  4. Remove chicken and vegetables, keep warm. Reduce sauce until slightly thickened. Off heat, whisk in cold butter. Adjust seasoning.
  5. Arrange chicken and vegetables on a platter, spoon sauce over, sprinkle with parsley. Serve with mashed potatoes, buttered noodles, or crusty bread.

📊 Nutrition (per serving)

~480 cal, 42g protein, 28g fat, 10g carbs.
