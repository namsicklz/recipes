Italian Grinder Potato Salad

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 lbs baby potatoes
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 12 oz grape tomatoes, halved
  • ¼ red onion, finely diced
  • 7 oz salami, diced into ½-inch pieces
  • 8 oz bocconcini pearls (mini mozzarella balls)
  • 4 oz smoky hot Colby Jack cheese, diced
  • 20 peppadew peppers, roughly chopped

Creamy Pepperoncini Dressing

  • ⅓ cup olive oil
  • ¼ cup pepperoncini brine
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • 3 tbsp Greek yogurt or sour cream
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmigiano Reggiano

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Boil potatoes in salted water ~10 minutes until fork tender. Drain, halve, cool slightly.
  2. Blend all dressing ingredients until smooth and creamy.
  3. Combine potatoes, parsley, tomatoes, red onion, salami, bocconcini, cheese, and peppadew peppers in a large bowl.
  4. Pour dressing over, toss gently. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes before serving.

💡 Chef Tips

Use warm potatoes when dressing for better flavor absorption; add extra pepperoncini brine for more tang. Excellent meal-prep salad.

Healthier version

Turkey salami instead of regular, reduced-fat cheese, extra tomatoes + parsley, half potatoes / half cauliflower.
