Shrimp & Zucchini Pasta Salad

Nutrient-dense, high-protein weekday meal with a mint vinaigrette.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 12 oz kale, finely chopped
  • 1½ cups cooked couscous or quinoa
  • 1½ cups frozen peas, thawed
  • 1 bundle chives, finely chopped
  • ¼ cup mint, parsley, or basil, finely chopped
  • 6 wooden skewers, soaked in water 15 minutes
  • 18 shrimp, shells removed and cleaned
  • 2 large zucchini, cut into ½-inch moons
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • Pepper to taste

Mint Vinaigrette

  • ⅓ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • ¼ cup orange juice
  • ¾ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp mustard
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • ¼ cup mint, parsley, or tarragon

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Add kale, couscous or quinoa, peas, chives, and mint to a large serving bowl.
  2. Blend all vinaigrette ingredients; dress the salad and toss to combine.
  3. Preheat the grill to high. Skewer the shrimp and zucchini, brush with olive oil, season with salt and pepper. Grill 2 minutes per side.
  4. Add shrimp and zucchini to the salad, toss once more.
