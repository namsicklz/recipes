Michelin Chicken Pesto Orzo

One-pan chicken, blistered cherry tomatoes, mushrooms, and orzo in a silky pesto-broth sauce.

Serves 2–3Total Time 30–35 minutes

🧂 Ingredients

Chicken & Veg

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • Kosher salt & cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 2–3 garlic cloves, minced

Orzo & Sauce

  • 1 cup orzo
  • 1¾ cups chicken broth
  • 2–3 tbsp pesto (adjust to taste)
  • 1–2 tbsp grated Parmesan (optional)
  • Squeeze of lemon

To Finish

  • Extra pesto, black pepper, a drizzle of olive oil

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Pat chicken thighs dry, season generously with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large sauté pan over medium-high heat. Sear thighs 4–5 minutes per side until deep golden. Remove and set aside (they finish cooking in the orzo).
  2. In the same pan, add butter. Sauté sliced onion 2 minutes until softened. Add mushrooms, sauté until golden, 3–4 minutes. Add cherry tomatoes, cook until blistered and jammy, 2 minutes. Add garlic, sauté 20–30 seconds until fragrant — don't brown it.
  3. Add dry orzo directly to the vegetable mixture, stir 1–2 minutes until lightly toasted.
  4. Pour in the broth, scraping up all the browned bits. Nestle the seared chicken thighs into the broth. Lower heat to medium, simmer 10–12 minutes, stirring orzo occasionally, until orzo is tender and chicken is cooked through (thighs should reach 175–185°F/79–85°C — pull the initial sear at 155–160°F so they finish gently here).
  5. Once orzo is al dente, stir in the pesto, a squeeze of lemon, and Parmesan if using. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, pesto. Don't cook the pesto long — stir it in at the end so the basil stays vibrant.

Plating

Spoon a bed of pesto-orzo in the center of the plate, place chicken thighs slightly overlapping, scatter blistered tomatoes and mushrooms around, drizzle a thin line of pesto mixed with olive oil, finish with cracked pepper, Parmesan, and fresh basil leaves.

💡 Chef Tips

If the sauce tightens too much, add a splash of broth at the end. For restaurant gloss, swirl in ½ tbsp cold butter right before serving (monter au beurre). Always finish pesto dishes with lemon, not salt.
