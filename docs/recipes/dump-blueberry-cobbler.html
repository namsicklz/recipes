Dump Blueberry Cobbler

🧂 Ingredients

  • 250g blueberries
  • 2 tbsp light brown sugar
  • Zest of ½ lemon

Cobbler mix

  • 125g self-raising flour
  • 75g caster sugar
  • 120ml milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 60g butter
  • Pinch of salt

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Melt butter in a pan over low heat until bubbling, then add the rest of the cobbler mix ingredients, stir loosely, and turn off the heat. Lumps are fine.
  2. Add blueberries and stir through.
  3. Add extra blueberries on top along with sugar and lemon zest.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 35 minutes.
  5. Finish with ice cream and a pinch of flaky sea salt.
