Salted Caramel Apple Campfire Pies

Source: Tasty. Makes 4 servings, cooked in a cast-iron pie iron over campfire coals.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
  • 8 brioche bread slices
  • Cooking spray
  • 1½ cups homemade or store-bought apple pie filling
  • 4 tsp caramel sauce (thicker style works best)
  • ½ tsp flaky sea salt

Instructions

  1. Spread butter evenly on one side of each bread slice.
  2. Lightly coat a 4-inch cast-iron pie iron with cooking spray. Preheat over medium-hot campfire coals for 1 minute.
  3. Place 1 bread slice, buttered side down, in the bottom of the hot pie iron. Spoon ~3 tbsp apple pie filling onto the center, spread evenly leaving a ½-inch border. Drizzle 1 tsp caramel sauce over the filling, sprinkle lightly with flaky sea salt. Top with another bread slice, buttered side facing out. Close and clamp the pie iron shut.
  4. Cook directly over campfire coals (not flames) 4–6 minutes, flipping occasionally, until bread is golden brown and crisp. Check for doneness with heat-resistant gloves — bread golden, filling hot and gooey.
  5. Transfer to a plate, let cool 1–2 minutes before serving (filling will be very hot). Repeat with remaining ingredients.

Notes

Can also be made in an air fryer per reader feedback on the source recipe.
