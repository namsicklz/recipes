Homemade Fluffy Marshmallows

Source: Life With Tuyen. Small batch — feel free to double.

Ingredients

Syrup

  • ⅔ cup (135g) granulated sugar
  • ⅓ cup (80ml) light corn syrup
  • 2 tbsp water

Gelatin Base

  • ¼ cup (60ml) water
  • 1 packet unflavored gelatin
  • 1–2 tsp vanilla extract or vanilla bean paste
  • Optional: dash of salt

Coating

  • Powdered sugar + cornstarch mix, for dusting

Instructions

  1. Bloom the gelatin: add the ¼ cup water and gelatin to a bowl, let sit while making the syrup.
  2. Combine sugar, corn syrup, and 2 tbsp water in a saucepan. Heat slowly over medium until sugar dissolves, then bring to a boil.
  3. Using a candy thermometer, boil until syrup hits 240°F (115°C). Without a thermometer, boil at a steady simmer ~1 minute after everything is melted.
  4. Remove syrup from heat. Slowly pour the hot syrup into the gelatin bowl while whisking.
  5. Whisk on high speed ~8 minutes until thick, glossy, and shiny. Halfway through, add vanilla and a dash of salt.
  6. Grease or oil a pan, dust with powdered sugar + cornstarch.
  7. Pour marshmallow mixture in, smooth the top, dust again with the sugar/cornstarch mix. Let set ~4 hours or overnight.
  8. Cut into cubes using a lightly oiled knife dusted with the powdered sugar blend.

Tips

Ensure syrup reaches soft-ball stage (240°F) for a fluffy texture. Add ingredients slowly and keep pot sides clean to avoid crystals. Store in an airtight bag with extra powdered sugar mix — keeps ~2–3 weeks cool/dry, or 3–4 weeks refrigerated.
