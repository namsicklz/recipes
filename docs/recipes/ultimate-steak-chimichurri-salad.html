Ultimate Steak Chimichurri Salad

A protein- and fiber-packed salad that's better as it sits — great for meal prep since there's no lettuce to go soggy.

Ingredients

Chimichurri

  • ⅓ cup olive oil
  • ¼ cup cilantro, chopped
  • ¼ cup parsley, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Salad

  • 1½ lbs steak of choice, cooked as desired (ribeye, sirloin, flank, or NY strip)
  • 6 Persian cucumbers, chopped
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans, drained & rinsed
  • ½ red onion, sliced thinly
  • ½ cup shaved/sliced parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Mix all chimichurri ingredients in a small bowl; taste and adjust.
  2. Season steak generously with salt and pepper. Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until very hot. Sear steak 3 minutes per side (adjust for thickness/doneness). Rest 10 minutes, then cube or slice.
  3. Combine cucumbers, tomatoes, garbanzo beans, red onion, and parmesan in a large bowl. Pour chimichurri over, toss well. Top with steak.

Chef Tips

Flank steak is leaner for health goals. Let salad sit 15–20 minutes before serving for best flavor. Add avocado if serving immediately.

Health notes

For blood sugar/cholesterol/heart-health goals: use sirloin or flank instead of ribeye, reduce parmesan slightly, add extra chickpeas + cucumber, serve over arugula or kale.
