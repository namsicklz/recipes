Trader Joe's Pesto Chicken — Three Ways

Three ways to dress up Trader Joe's Pesto Genovese Chicken Breast.

1. Tuscan Pesto Chicken with Lemon-Parmesan Orzo (Elegant & Easy)

The pesto's richness needs acidity and starch to balance — orzo absorbs the sauce, lemon keeps it bright.

Ingredients

  • 1 package Trader Joe's Pesto Chicken
  • 1 cup orzo
  • 2 tbsp olive oil + 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 cup chicken broth + ½ cup water
  • Zest and juice of ½ lemon
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan
  • Optional: handful baby spinach or arugula
  • Fresh basil or rosemary for garnish

Instructions

  1. Sear the pesto chicken in an oven-safe pan or skillet until browned (3–4 min per side). Transfer to a 375°F oven for 10–12 min until fully heated through.
  2. In the same pan, add butter and garlic. Add orzo, toast 1 minute, then pour in broth. Simmer until al dente and liquid absorbed (8–10 min).
  3. Stir in lemon zest, juice, and Parmesan. Taste for salt.
  4. Slice the chicken, place over the orzo, spoon pan juices on top. Garnish with fresh basil and a drizzle of olive oil.

Chef's touch: crushed pistachios or toasted pine nuts add crunch.

2. Pesto Chicken Flatbread (Modern Bistro Style)

Turns pre-cooked pesto chicken into a rustic flatbread — crisp, creamy, fresh.

Ingredients

  • Naan or pizza crust
  • Pesto chicken, sliced
  • Fresh mozzarella or burrata
  • Sliced cherry tomatoes
  • A few drops of balsamic glaze
  • Arugula tossed in lemon and olive oil

Instructions

  1. Brush naan with olive oil, toast at 425°F for 3–5 minutes.
  2. Layer mozzarella and chicken, bake until cheese melts.
  3. Add tomatoes and drizzle with balsamic glaze.
  4. Top with dressed arugula just before serving.

Chef's touch: brush the crust edge with a garlic-olive oil emulsion and sprinkle flaky sea salt after baking.

3. Creamy Pesto Chicken Tagliatelle (Restaurant Comfort)

The pesto flavor melts into cream for a rich, velvety pasta sauce.

Ingredients

  • Pesto chicken, sliced
  • 8 oz tagliatelle or fettuccine
  • 1 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan
  • ½ tsp cracked pepper
  • ½ tsp lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta in salted water until just al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water.
  2. Warm butter and olive oil in a skillet. Add chicken slices, sauté briefly.
  3. Pour in cream, Parmesan, and a splash of pasta water; stir until silky.
  4. Add pasta, toss well. Finish with lemon juice and pepper.

Chef's touch: sautéed mushrooms or sun-dried tomatoes add depth and color contrast.
