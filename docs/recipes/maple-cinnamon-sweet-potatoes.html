Maple Cinnamon Sweet Potatoes

A healthy Thanksgiving side dish.

Ingredients

  • Sweet potatoes, halved
  • EVOO
  • Maple syrup
  • Ground cinnamon
  • Sea salt

Instructions

  1. Coat sweet potatoes in EVOO, maple syrup, cinnamon, and sea salt.
  2. Place face down on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  3. Bake at 400°F for 40 minutes.
  4. Flip and broil a few minutes to caramelize.
