The Best Classic Shepherd's Pie

Source: thewholesomedish.com

🧂 Ingredients (serves 6)

Meat Filling:

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup chopped yellow onion
  • 1 lb ground beef (90% lean) or ground lamb
  • 2 tsp dried parsley leaves
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary leaves
  • 1 tsp dried thyme leaves
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 cup frozen mixed peas & carrots
  • 1/2 cup frozen corn kernels

Potato Topping:

  • 1 1/2–2 lb russet potatoes (about 2 large), peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 8 tbsp unsalted butter (1 stick)
  • 1/3 cup half & half
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1/4 cup parmesan cheese

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat; sauté onion about 5 minutes.
  2. Add ground beef or lamb, parsley, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper. Cook until browned, 6–8 minutes.
  3. Stir in garlic and Worcestershire sauce; cook 1 minute. Add flour and tomato paste, stir to coat.
  4. Gradually add beef broth, then stir in frozen peas & carrots and corn. Simmer until the gravy thickens, about 5 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes until tender; drain. Mash with butter, half & half, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and parmesan.
  6. Spread the meat filling in a baking dish; top evenly with the mashed potatoes.
  7. Bake at 400°F for 25–30 minutes. Let sit 15 minutes before serving.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~6 servings)

~450 cal, 24g protein, 38g carbs, 25g fat.
