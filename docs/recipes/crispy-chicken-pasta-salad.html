Crispy Chicken Pasta Salad

🧂 Ingredients (serves ~4)

Crispy Chicken

  • 400g boneless chicken, cubed
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • ½ tbsp salt
  • 1 cup plain flour
  • 1 tbsp corn flour

Avocado Sauce

  • 1 avocado
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • ½ cup yoghurt

Assembly

  • 1 cup chopped lettuce
  • ½ cup cherry tomatoes
  • ½ cup chopped apples
  • ½ cup chopped onions
  • ½ cup chopped cucumber
  • ½ cup chopped spring onions (optional)
  • ½ cup sweet corn
  • 1 cup boiled pasta
  • 1 cup crispy air-fried chicken
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • Handful chopped mint (optional)
  • Salt to taste (for veggies)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Season chicken cubes with paprika, garlic powder, oregano, black pepper, salt. Coat in flour + corn flour mix. Air fry or bake at 200°C (400°F) for 15–20 minutes until golden and crispy.
  2. Blend avocado, garlic, chilli flakes, salt, lemon juice, and yoghurt until smooth for the sauce.
  3. Combine lettuce, tomatoes, apples, onions, cucumber, spring onions, sweet corn, and pasta in a large bowl. Add crispy chicken, drizzle with avocado sauce.
  4. Toss lightly, sprinkle chilli flakes and mint, adjust seasoning. Serve immediately for best texture.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~460 cal, 32g protein, 42g carbs, 18g fat.
