Bourbon Apple Cider Punch

Source: Foxes Love Lemons. Fizzy, festive fall party punch.

Yield: ~10 servings · Prep: ~10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 bottle Champagne or sparkling wine (750mL)
  • 4 cups apple cider (32 oz)
  • 3 cups ginger ale (24 oz, Vernor's for classic Detroit flavor)
  • ½ cup bourbon (4 oz)
  • Sliced apples, for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. Stir together Champagne/sparkling wine, apple cider, ginger ale, and bourbon in a large pitcher or punch bowl.
  2. Serve over ice.
  3. Garnish with apple slices if desired.

Tips

Great as a big-batch cocktail for holidays or parties. Substitute rum or another spirit if preferred. Add cinnamon sticks or star anise for extra autumn flair.
