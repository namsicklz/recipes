Cajun Potato Bricks with Honey Mustard BBQ Sauce

Ingredients (serves ~6)

Potatoes

  • 2–3 lbs Russet potatoes
  • 3 tbsp avocado oil (Chosen Foods recommended — handles high heat well)
  • 8 tbsp butter, melted
  • 3 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 3 tbsp Parmesan cheese, grated
  • Fresh chives, chopped, for garnish

Sauce

  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp BBQ sauce
  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • Water, to thin as needed

Instructions

  1. Cut potatoes into ¼-inch thick planks, crosshatch lightly with skewers and a knife. Cut into 4 pieces, soak in cold water 20–30 minutes to draw out starch. Drain and pat dry.
  2. Preheat oven or smoker to 425°F (220°C). Spray or drizzle sheet pan with avocado oil, place potatoes cut-side down.
  3. Mix melted butter with Cajun seasoning, brush generously over potatoes. Bake ~30 minutes until golden and crispy.
  4. Whisk sauce ingredients together, thinning with water to desired consistency.
  5. Drizzle sauce over potatoes, sprinkle Parmesan, garnish with chives.

Nutrition (per serving, ~6 servings)

~365 kcal, 5g protein, 38g carbs, 22g fat (estimates vary with exact butter/oil amounts).
