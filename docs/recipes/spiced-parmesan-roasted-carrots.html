Spiced Parmesan Roasted Carrots

Ingredients

  • 1 lb carrots, sliced into 3" long flat pieces
  • ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • ½ cup grated Parmigiano Reggiano, split into 2 batches of ¼ cup
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • Maldon salt, to taste

Yogurt Dressing

  • 4 tbsp plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 garlic clove, pressed
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • ¼ cup milk

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Mix onion powder, cumin, paprika, salt, chilli flakes, and olive oil in a bowl. Coat carrot pieces, add ¼ cup Parmigiano Reggiano, toss.
  2. Place carrots evenly on a baking tray, add a layer of Parmigiano Reggiano on top. Bake 20–25 minutes until tender.
  3. Remove, plate, sprinkle with Maldon salt.
  4. Whisk yogurt dressing ingredients until smooth. Drizzle over carrots, finish with a sprinkle of paprika and cumin.
