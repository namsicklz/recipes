20-Minute Wonton Soup (with Chili Oil)

Source: Brita Cooks

🧂 Ingredients

Wonton Soup

  • 3 green onions, chopped (separate whites + light green from dark green tops)
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, minced or grated
  • 4 cups broth (chicken, beef, or vegetable)
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil (or neutral oil)
  • 12 oz frozen wontons (e.g. chicken cilantro mini wontons)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1–2 dashes fish sauce
  • Optional: dash of rice wine vinegar

Aunt Lily's Chili Oil

  • ½ cup neutral oil (peanut, avocado, vegetable, canola)
  • 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)
  • ¼ cup red pepper flakes
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, minced
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Optional: cinnamon stick

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Chili oil: Combine red pepper flakes, sesame seeds, garlic, ginger, sugar, salt (and cinnamon if using) in a heat-safe bowl. Heat neutral oil until hot (drop in some garlic — if it sizzles, it's ready). Carefully pour hot oil over the mixture, stir to infuse.
  2. Soup: Melt coconut oil in a pot. Add whites and light green parts of green onions, garlic, and ginger; sauté until fragrant. Pour in broth, simmer ~5 minutes.
  3. Add wontons, soy sauce, fish sauce, and optional rice wine vinegar. Simmer until wontons are heated through.
  4. Ladle into bowls, top with dark green onion parts, drizzle with chili oil to taste.
