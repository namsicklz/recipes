Dutch Baby Pancake

Source: [Sally's Baking Addiction](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/dutch-baby-pancake-recipe/). Serves 4-6. Sweet version, with a savory sour cream & chive variation in Notes below.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 3/4 cup (94g) all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup (180ml) whole milk, at room temperature (or nondairy)
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest
  • 2 Tablespoons (25g) granulated sugar
  • pinch salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 3 Tablespoons (43g) unsalted butter

Toppings: 2 Tablespoons confectioners' sugar, for sifting; optional berries, whipped cream, maple syrup, and/or strawberry sauce.

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Place the eggs in a blender and blend on medium-high speed until frothy and fluffy, about 45 seconds.
  2. Add flour, milk, lemon zest, sugar, salt, and vanilla, and blend until combined. The batter is thin.
  3. Set aside and allow to rest for 15 minutes at room temperature, or cover and refrigerate up to 24 hours (no need to bring to room temperature before baking).
  4. Preheat the oven to 400°F (204°C). Place butter in a 10-inch oven-safe skillet (or any 3-quart baking dish) and place in the oven for 5 minutes to melt the butter — don't let it burn.
  5. Remove from the oven and use a pastry brush to spread the melted butter up the sides of the pan.
  6. Pour the batter into the center of the hot pan — no need to swirl it around.
  7. Bake for 15 minutes, then reduce oven temperature to 350°F (177°C) (leave pancake in the oven) and bake 10 more minutes, until the edges and center are browned.
  8. Remove from the oven and cool 5 minutes before adding toppings. Slice and serve immediately for best results.
  9. Leftovers keep covered in the fridge up to 5 days, or frozen up to 2 months.

📝 Notes

  • Milk: whole milk preferred, but any dairy or nondairy milk works.
  • Lemon zest: adds subtle brightness, not a strong lemon flavor. Add more for stronger flavor, skip entirely, or swap in 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon.
  • No cast iron skillet: use any ~10-inch oven-safe skillet, a 3-quart baking dish, a 10-inch pie dish, or a 9-inch square baking pan.
  • Savory version (sour cream & chive): reduce sugar to 1 Tablespoon, increase salt to 1/4 teaspoon. Add 2 Tablespoons chopped fresh chives, 2 teaspoons chopped fresh parsley, 1 Tablespoon sour cream, 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder, and 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper. Continue with the recipe as written. Top with pickled red onions, halved cherry tomatoes, smoked salmon, more sour cream, arugula, and more chives/parsley.
