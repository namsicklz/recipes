Fermented Strawberry Soda

Source: @revolveprimalhealth. Beginner-friendly — no starter, SCOBY, or ginger bug required.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 1½ cups chopped strawberries (or strawberry scraps)
  • 5 cups water
  • ½ cup maple syrup
  • Juice of 1 lemon (optional)
  • 2 x 32 oz glass mason jars
  • 2 flip-top bottles

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Combine: Divide strawberries, water, maple syrup, and lemon juice evenly into two 32 oz mason jars (or one large jar). Stir well to dissolve the maple syrup.
  2. Cover: Cover each jar with a breathable cloth secured with a rubber band.
  3. Ferment: Leave at room temperature 2–3 days, stirring once daily, until bubbles form and it smells fruity and slightly tangy.
  4. Strain & bottle: Strain out the strawberry solids. Pour liquid into clean flip-top bottles, leaving 1–2 inches of headspace.
  5. Carbonate: Seal, leave at room temperature 1–3 days to build carbonation, "burping" the bottles once a day to release excess pressure.
  6. Chill & serve: Once fizzy enough, transfer to the fridge. Serve cold over ice.

Safety Tip

Burp daily during carbonation to avoid pressure buildup. Once ready, refrigerate 4+ hours (or overnight) before opening. Cold beverages retain carbonation and reduce pressure risk.

📊 Nutrition (per 8 oz serving, approx.)

70 cal, 13g sugar, 16g carbs, 0g fat, 0g protein, 5mg sodium.
