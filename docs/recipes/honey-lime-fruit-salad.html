Honey Lime Fruit Salad

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sliced strawberries
  • 1 cup sliced kiwi
  • 1 cup blackberries
  • 1 cup raspberries
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 1 cup halved grapes
  • 1½ tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp lime zest
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 3 tbsp fresh chopped mint

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, combine mint, lime zest, lime juice, and honey for the dressing.
  2. In a separate bowl, combine all the fruit.
  3. Top fruit with dressing, toss well, and enjoy.
