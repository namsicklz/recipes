Mulligatawny Soup

Classic Anglo-Indian soup — fragrant, velvety, lightly spiced with curry. Serves 8.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 3 cups chopped vegetables (mixture of onions, carrots, celery, parsnips, mushrooms)
  • 6 cups homemade meat broth (or vegetable/chicken broth)
  • 1 tsp finely chopped garlic (about 1 clove)
  • 1 sprig fresh coriander leaves (or 1 tbsp dry coriander leaves)
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp usli ghee (or light vegetable oil)
  • ½ cup finely chopped onions
  • 4 tsp curry powder
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • Kosher salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp finely minced fresh coriander leaves (or 1 tbsp dry)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Combine chopped vegetables, broth, garlic, coriander, and black pepper in a deep, heavy-bottomed 3-quart saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover, simmer 45 minutes until vegetables are soft. Turn off heat.
  2. When slightly cool, puree the soup (broth and vegetables) in a blender or food processor. Pass through a fine sieve for a smooth, velvety texture. Return to the pan, bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. While simmering, heat ghee and chopped onions in a small frying pan over medium-high, stirring constantly, until onions turn caramel brown (~10 min). Add curry powder and flour, cook 1 minute stirring rapidly. Turn off heat.
  4. Add the onion-spice mixture into the simmering soup, whisking constantly to prevent lumps. Simmer until slightly thickened (~2 min).
  5. Stir in heavy cream and additional coriander leaves. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. Serve hot.

💡 Chef's Notes

Can be prepared ahead: refrigerate up to 3 days or freeze up to 1 month. Defrost and heat gently. Traditionally served with rice or naan.
