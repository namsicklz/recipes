Chicken Kefta with Roasted Garlic Paprika Sauce

Reconstructed recipe — the original was only ever saved as a generated PDF that's no longer recoverable. This is a plausible version based on the name, not the original. Makes 4 servings.

🧂 Ingredients

Kefta

  • 1½ lb ground chicken
  • 1 small onion, grated and squeezed dry
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Roasted Garlic Paprika Sauce

  • 1 whole head garlic, roasted
  • ½ cup plain yogurt or sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt, to taste

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Roast the garlic: slice the top off the head, drizzle with olive oil, wrap in foil, and roast at 400°F (200°C) for 35–40 minutes until soft and golden. Let cool, then squeeze cloves from their skins.
  2. Combine all kefta ingredients in a bowl and mix gently until just combined (don't overwork). Shape into 8 oblong patties or mold onto skewers.
  3. Chill the kefta for 20 minutes to help them hold their shape.
  4. Grill or pan-sear over medium-high heat, 3–4 minutes per side, until cooked through (165°F internal).
  5. For the sauce, mash the roasted garlic into a paste and whisk together with yogurt, mayonnaise, paprika, lemon juice, and salt.
  6. Serve the kefta warm with the sauce spooned over or alongside.

📝 Notes

"Kefta" (also spelled kofta) refers to spiced ground-meat kebabs common across Middle Eastern and Mediterranean cooking — traditionally lamb or beef, here made with chicken.
