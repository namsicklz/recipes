Beer Pizza Dough

Reconstructed recipe — the original ("award-winning" per the source chat) was only ever saved as a generated PDF that's no longer recoverable. This is a plausible standard beer pizza dough, not the original. Makes 2 medium pizza crusts.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 12 oz beer (a lager or pale ale works well), room temperature
  • 3½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2¼ tsp (1 packet) active dry yeast
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, plus more for the bowl

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Warm the beer slightly (should feel just-warm, not hot) and stir in the sugar and yeast. Let sit 5–10 minutes until foamy.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour and salt. Make a well in the center, pour in the beer mixture and olive oil.
  3. Mix until a shaggy dough forms, then knead on a floured surface for 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place in an oiled bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot for 1–1.5 hours, until doubled in size.
  5. Punch down, divide into 2 balls, and let rest 10 minutes before rolling out.
  6. Top as desired and bake at 475°F (245°C) on a preheated pizza stone or sheet pan for 10–14 minutes, until golden and crisp.

📝 Notes

The beer's yeast and carbonation give the crust a slightly tangy, airy texture compared to a plain water-based dough.
