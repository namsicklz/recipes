Homemade Twizzlers

Real-fruit version of the classic candy — stretchy, chewy, and strawberry-flavored, with no corn syrup. Source: Crowded Kitchen.

Prep 15 minCook ~20–25 minDehydrate 4.5–5 hrs at 140°F (or 3–4 hrs in oven at 175°F, door cracked)Yield ~20–24 twizzlers

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 cups hulled strawberries (250g)
  • Juice of ½ large lemon (~1 tbsp)
  • 1 cup granulated sugar (200g)
  • 2 cups cake flour (240g)
  • 1½ cups cold water
  • 2–3 tbsp freeze-dried strawberries, ground and strained (6–10g)
  • Natural red food coloring (optional)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Blend strawberries, lemon juice, and water; strain out the seeds.
  2. Simmer the strawberry purée with sugar for about 10 minutes, until thickened.
  3. Whisk cake flour, cold water, and strawberry powder together until smooth.
  4. Add the flour mixture to the syrup in ¼ cup batches, stirring to combine; add food coloring if using.
  5. Cook on medium-low, stirring constantly, until the dough resembles glossy play-dough.
  6. Cool briefly on a nonstick surface.
  7. Roll into 1-tablespoon portions, shape into ¼-inch strands, and twist together.
  8. Dehydrate or bake until chewy but not tough.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Add the flour slurry in small batches to prevent clumping.
  • Keep stirring constantly to avoid scorching.
  • The dough looks clumpy partway through — trust the process, it smooths out into a glossy texture.
  • Store airtight at room temperature for up to 4 days.
