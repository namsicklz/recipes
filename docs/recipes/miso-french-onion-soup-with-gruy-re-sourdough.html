Miso French Onion Soup with Gruyère & Sourdough

Source: Brita Cooks

Prep: ~10 min · Cook: ~40 min · Serves: ~3

Ingredients

  • 3–4 yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt (for onions)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (optional)
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 3 cups bone broth (or beef/chicken broth)
  • 2 tbsp white miso paste
  • Few sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Sourdough bread, cut to fit bowls, toasted
  • Grated Gruyère (or Comté or another melty cheese)
  • Garnish: extra fresh thyme

Instructions

  1. Melt butter with olive oil in a large pot. Add sliced onions and a pinch of salt. Sauté on medium-low ~20 minutes until golden brown and caramelized. Add minced garlic near the end if using.
  2. Deglaze with white wine, scraping up browned bits.
  3. Add bone broth, miso paste, thyme sprigs, and bay leaf. Simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Remove thyme and bay leaf. Ladle soup into oven-safe bowls.
  5. Top each bowl with toasted sourdough, sprinkle generously with grated Gruyère.
  6. Broil until cheese is golden and bubbly. Garnish with fresh thyme, serve hot.
