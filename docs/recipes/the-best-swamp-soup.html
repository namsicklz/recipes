The Best Swamp Soup

Source: Allrecipes. Makes 8 servings, ~55 minutes.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 1 lb smoked sausage (such as Conecuh), cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 cup finely chopped onion
  • 2 jalapeños, seeded and chopped
  • 4 gold potatoes, diced
  • 1 (28 oz) can seasoned turnip greens
  • 1 (14 oz) can hoppin' john or seasoned black-eyed peas
  • 1 (10 oz) can diced tomatoes and green chiles
  • 1 can navy beans, drained
  • 2 tsp chicken bouillon paste (such as Better Than Bouillon), or 2 chicken bouillon cubes
  • 2 cups water

📊 Nutrition (per serving)

~294 cal, 11g protein, 14g carbs, 9g fat.

📝 Notes

Allrecipes blocks automated fetching (the publisher has opted out even third-party reader tools), so the exact numbered steps weren't pulled — ingredients and nutrition above came from search results and a mirror site. Pull the full step-by-step from allrecipes.com's "The Best Swamp Soup" page if remaking without them; based on the ingredient list this reads as a standard dump-and-simmer soup (brown the sausage, sauté onion and jalapeño, add the rest, simmer).
