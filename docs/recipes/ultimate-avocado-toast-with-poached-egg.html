Ultimate Avocado Toast with Poached Egg

Restaurant-brunch level — crunchy bread, creamy avocado, bright acid, rich egg yolk, layers of texture.

Serves: 2 (large servings)

🧂 Ingredients

Base

  • 2 thick slices sourdough
  • Olive oil
  • Flaky salt

Avocado Mash

  • 2 ripe avocados
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Small garlic clove, microplaned
  • Salt + pepper
  • Pinch red pepper flakes

Toppings

  • 2 poached eggs
  • Crumbled feta or cotija
  • Thin sliced pickled red onions
  • Chili crisp or Calabrian chili oil
  • Microgreens or arugula
  • Everything bagel seasoning
  • Optional: crispy prosciutto, smoked salmon, hot honey

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Toast: Brush sourdough lightly with olive oil, toast until deeply golden and crisp — the crunch matters.
  2. Avocado: Mash avocado with lemon, garlic, salt, pepper, and chili flakes. Leave it slightly chunky.
  3. Poach the eggs: Bring water to a gentle simmer, add a splash of vinegar. Crack eggs into small bowls first, then slide into the water. Cook 3 minutes for runny, 4 minutes for jammy. Drain on a paper towel.
  4. Build: Layer avocado, feta, poached egg, pickled onions, chili crisp, microgreens, everything seasoning, flaky salt.

The Secret Upgrade

A thin swipe of whipped feta underneath the avocado, or crispy shallots on top.

Best Version

Sourdough + whipped feta + avocado + jammy poached egg + chili crunch + pickled onions + microgreens.
