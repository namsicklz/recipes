Cucumber, Tomato & Avocado Salad

Inspired by a Tasty recipe.

🧂 Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 1 large cucumber, sliced
  • 1–2 avocados, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved (or diced regular tomatoes)
  • ¼ cup thinly sliced red onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • Fresh herbs (cilantro, dill, or parsley), chopped — optional
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Combine cucumber, tomatoes, onions, avocado, and herbs in a large bowl.
  2. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice.
  3. Toss gently to coat evenly.
  4. Season with salt and pepper. Serve immediately for peak freshness.

📊 Nutrition (per serving)

~260 kcal, 22g fat, 17g carbs, 3g protein.
