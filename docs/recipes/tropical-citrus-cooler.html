Tropical Citrus Cooler

A refreshing, hydrating, tangy-sweet juice.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 1 cup watermelon (cubed)
  • 1 kiwi (peeled)
  • ½ cup pineapple (cubed)
  • ½ apple (any kind, cored)
  • ½ cucumber (peeled if waxed)
  • ½ lime (juiced)
  • ½ lemon (juiced)
  • Ice (optional)
  • Mint leaves (optional, for garnish)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Add watermelon, kiwi, pineapple, apple, and cucumber to a blender or juicer.
  2. Squeeze in the lime and lemon juice.
  3. Blend until smooth. If using a blender, strain through a fine mesh or cheesecloth if desired.
  4. Serve over ice, garnish with mint.
