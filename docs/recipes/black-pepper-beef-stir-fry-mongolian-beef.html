Black Pepper Beef Stir Fry (Mongolian Beef)

🧂 Ingredients (serves 4)

Beef Marinade

  • 650g beef, thin strips (~3")
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • ½ tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp water

Stir Fry

  • 4–5 tbsp oil
  • 5–6 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 handful spring onions, chopped
  • 1 large white onion, sliced
  • ⅓ cup soy sauce
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Handful green spring onion tops, for garnish

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Combine beef strips with oil, salt, baking soda, black pepper, paprika, cornflour, and water. Mix well, marinate 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a wok over high heat. Stir-fry beef until browned and nearly cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same wok, add garlic and onions, sauté until fragrant and softened.
  4. Add soy sauce, sugar, black pepper; stir to combine.
  5. Return beef to wok, toss to coat evenly. Add spring onions, cook 2–3 minutes until glossy and cooked through.
  6. Garnish with green spring onion tops. Serve hot with steamed rice or noodles.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~420 cal, 32g protein, 16g carbs, 25g fat.
