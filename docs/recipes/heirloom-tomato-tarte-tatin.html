Heirloom Tomato Tarte Tatin

A savory upside-down tart, best in late summer when heirloom tomatoes peak.

🧂 Ingredients

  • Heirloom tomatoes
  • Shallots
  • Garlic cloves
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Basil
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • ½ cup balsamic vinegar
  • Puff pastry sheet
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: honey, egg wash

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Spread tomatoes, garlic, shallots, and a pinch of salt and pepper in a medium pan.
  2. Cook over medium heat with olive oil and balsamic vinegar until reduced — let it simmer without stirring until juices are minimal and fully reduced.
  3. Add a drizzle of honey, then top with a puff pastry sheet, poking holes with a fork throughout (optional: coat with egg wash before baking for a nicer finish).
  4. Bake at 400°F for about 20 minutes or until pastry is golden.
  5. Carefully flip onto a plate, top with Parmesan cheese and basil.

🍽️ Serving Ideas

Pair with grilled chicken, steak, or salmon for dinner; serve with a peppery arugula salad for lunch; add burrata or fresh mozzarella after baking; finish with balsamic glaze and flaky sea salt.
