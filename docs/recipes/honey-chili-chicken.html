Honey Chili Chicken

🧂 Ingredients (serves ~6)

Chicken Marinade

  • 1 kg chicken drumsticks
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp cumin powder
  • 1 tbsp mixed dried herbs
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp onion powder
  • 1–2 tbsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • Juice of 1 lemon

Sticky Sauce

  • 80g butter
  • 1 red chilli, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, chopped
  • Handful chopped spring onions
  • Handful chopped coriander
  • ½ tbsp chilli powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ⅓ cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • ⅓ cup water (optional, for more sauce)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Combine marinade ingredients, coat drumsticks, refrigerate at least 1 hour (overnight best).
  2. Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 35–40 minutes until golden and cooked through (or grill for extra char).
  3. Melt butter, sauté garlic, chilli, spring onions until fragrant. Stir in soy sauce, honey, chilli powder, salt; thin with water if needed.
  4. Toss cooked chicken in sauce until coated. Garnish with coriander and extra spring onions.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~6 servings)

~385 cal, 32g protein, 12g carbs, 23g fat.
