Kids Chocolate Chip Protein Balls

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 cups peanut butter (or any nut/seed butter)
  • 1 cup honey
  • 3¾ cups oat flour (about 4 cups rolled oats blended into flour)
  • 1 tbsp vanilla extract
  • ½–1 cup chocolate chips (to taste)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Combine peanut butter, honey, oat flour, and vanilla in a large bowl. Mix by hand or with a mixer until a dough forms.
  2. If dough is too sticky, add more oat flour. If too dry, add more peanut butter.
  3. Once moldable (like play dough), fold in chocolate chips.
  4. Roll into small balls, about 1 inch wide.
  5. Optional: freeze half for later — great in lunchboxes, they thaw by lunchtime and stay cool.

🧊 Storage

Airtight container in the fridge up to 1 week, or freeze up to 3 months.

Healthier for cholesterol/blood sugar goals

Reduce honey, use natural peanut butter with no added sugar.
