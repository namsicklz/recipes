Miso Maple Mushroom Broth

Reconstructed recipe — the original was only ever saved as a generated PDF that's no longer recoverable. This is a plausible version based on the name — a savory-sweet mushroom broth built on miso and maple, in the same family as Miso French Onion Soup with Gruyère & Sourdough elsewhere in this collection. Makes about 6 cups.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 1-inch piece ginger, sliced
  • 8 oz mixed fresh mushrooms (shiitake, cremini), sliced
  • ½ oz dried shiitake mushrooms
  • 6 cups water or light vegetable stock
  • 3 tbsp white or yellow miso paste
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 green onion, sliced, for serving

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Sauté the onion, garlic, and ginger until fragrant, about 3–4 minutes.
  2. Add both fresh and dried mushrooms; cook 5 minutes until the fresh mushrooms release their liquid.
  3. Pour in the water or stock, bring to a simmer, and cook uncovered for 25–30 minutes to build flavor.
  4. Strain out the solids if you want a clear broth (or leave them in for a heartier soup).
  5. Whisk a ladleful of hot broth into the miso paste to dissolve it smoothly, then stir it back into the pot along with the maple syrup and soy sauce. Do not boil after adding miso (it dulls the flavor).
  6. Taste and adjust sweetness/saltiness. Serve topped with sliced green onion.

📝 Notes

Keeping the broth below a simmer once the miso goes in preserves its flavor and beneficial cultures.
