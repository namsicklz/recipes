Matthew McConaughey's Viral Tuna Salad

Sweet + savory + crunchy + spicy all at once.

Serves: 2–3

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 cans tuna (in oil or water), drained
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1–2 tbsp Italian dressing
  • 2–3 tbsp finely chopped red onion
  • 2–3 tbsp chopped pickles (or relish)
  • 1–2 tbsp chopped jalapeños (fresh or pickled)
  • 2 tbsp chopped apple
  • 1–2 tbsp chopped cornichons (optional upgrade)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt & black pepper to taste

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Combine tuna, mayo, mustard, and Italian dressing in a bowl.
  2. Mix in onion, pickles, jalapeños, and apple.
  3. Add honey and lemon juice.
  4. Salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Stir well, let sit 5–10 minutes so flavors come together.

Why it works

Apple + honey give subtle sweetness, pickles + mustard bring acidity, jalapeño brings heat, and Italian dressing adds the "secret" complexity.

How to eat it

Sandwich, lettuce wraps, crackers/toast, or stuffed in avocado.

Health tweaks

Olive-oil mayo or Greek yogurt swap, reduce honey to ½ tsp, add extra celery for fiber, use tuna in water, serve in lettuce cups.

💡 Chef upgrade

Add capers, use shallots instead of onion, finish with olive oil + flaky salt, add fresh parsley or dill.
