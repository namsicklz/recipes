Bacon-Wrapped Slow Cooker Pulled Pork

Inspired by a BuzzFeed recipe (Bourbon Bacon Pulled Pork).

🧂 Ingredients (serves ~8)

  • 2 lbs (900g) pork loin or shoulder
  • 8 slices bacon
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup bourbon (optional; substitute broth if desired)
  • ⅓ cup apple cider vinegar

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Rub pork with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Wrap with bacon slices.
  2. Place pork in the slow cooker. Mix brown sugar, bourbon, and apple cider vinegar; pour over the meat.
  3. Cover and cook on low 8–10 hours, until the pork shreds easily with a fork.
  4. Remove meat, shred, mix back into the cooking juices. Serve on buns, tacos, or alongside sides.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, 8 servings)

~380 cal, 23g protein, 28g carbs, 15g fat.
