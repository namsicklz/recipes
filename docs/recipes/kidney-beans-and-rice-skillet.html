Kidney Beans and Rice Skillet with Shawarma Spices

Source: theplantbasedschool.com. A simple, one-pan vegetarian dinner — sweet caramelized onions and warm shawarma spices over kidney beans and rice.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup water (added as needed while the onions cook, to prevent burning)
  • 1 tsp honey or sugar (helps the onions caramelize faster)
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 cans kidney beans
  • 2 cups cooked basmati rice (jasmine, brown rice, or leftover rice also work)
  • 2 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 3 tsp smoked or sweet paprika
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley

Yogurt sauce (for serving)

  • ½ cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 6 tbsp water
  • 1 pinch salt

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Add the sliced onions, honey, and a pinch of salt to a skillet. Cook over medium heat for about 15 minutes, stirring occasionally and adding splashes of water as needed to prevent burning, until soft and golden.
  2. Stir in the garlic and shawarma spices (coriander, cumin, paprika, cinnamon); cook briefly until fragrant.
  3. Add the kidney beans and cooked rice; stir to combine and heat through, finishing with olive oil, a squeeze of lemon, and salt to taste.
  4. Whisk together the yogurt sauce ingredients (Greek yogurt, lemon juice, water, salt) separately.
  5. Serve topped with the yogurt sauce and fresh parsley.
