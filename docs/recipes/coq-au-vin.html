Coq au Vin (Chicken Braised in Red Wine)

Classic French braise — chicken slow-cooked in red wine with bacon, mushrooms, and pearl onions until fork-tender.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 3–4 lbs chicken (bone-in thighs + drumsticks preferred)
  • Salt & black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 oz bacon or pancetta, diced
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 3–4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups dry red wine (Burgundy or Pinot Noir ideal)
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2–3 sprigs thyme
  • 8 oz mushrooms, halved
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp flour (optional, for thickening)
  • Fresh parsley, chopped

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Brown the bacon: Cook in a Dutch oven over medium heat until crisp. Remove, leave fat in pot.
  2. Sear the chicken: Pat dry, season well. Sear skin-side down until deeply golden (5–6 min), flip briefly, then remove.
  3. Build the base: In the same pot, add onion and carrots; cook 5–7 minutes until softened. Add garlic and tomato paste; cook 1–2 minutes.
  4. Deglaze: Pour in wine, scraping the bottom. Simmer 5 minutes to cook off alcohol.
  5. Braise: Add stock, thyme, bay leaves, bacon, and chicken. Liquid should come about ¾ up the chicken. Cover and simmer low (or 325°F oven) 45–60 minutes.
  6. Mushrooms: Sauté separately in butter until browned; set aside (keeps them from getting soggy).
  7. Finish the sauce: Remove chicken. Optional: whisk 1 tbsp flour into butter, or just reduce sauce until slightly thickened.
  8. Combine: Return chicken and mushrooms to pot, spoon sauce over, finish with parsley.

🍽️ Serving

Mashed potatoes, buttered noodles, or crusty bread.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Use good wine — you'll taste it.
  • Bone-in chicken beats boneless for flavor.
  • Even better made a day ahead; skim fat before serving.

Health tweaks

Skinless thighs, half the bacon, serve with cauliflower mash or greens, skip the flour to thicken naturally instead.
