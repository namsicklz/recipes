Garlic Herb Parmesan Roasted Potatoes

Reconstructed recipe — the original was only ever saved as a generated PDF that's no longer recoverable. This is a plausible version based on the name, in the same style as Crispy Roast Potatoes with Goose Fat & Herbs and Garlic & Rosemary Roast Potatoes elsewhere in this collection. Serves ~6.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 lbs baby or Yukon gold potatoes, halved
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried Italian herbs (or a mix of thyme, oregano, rosemary)
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped, for serving

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toss the halved potatoes with olive oil, garlic, dried herbs, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan, spreading them cut-side down.
  3. Roast 25 minutes, then flip and roast another 15–20 minutes until golden and fork-tender.
  4. Remove from the oven, sprinkle Parmesan evenly over the hot potatoes, and return to the oven for 3–5 minutes until the cheese melts and lightly browns.
  5. Top with fresh parsley before serving.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~6 servings)

~210 kcal, 6g protein, 27g carbs, 9g fat.
