Smashed Cucumber & Feta Salad

Source: The Kitchn

🧂 Ingredients

Vegetables

  • 2–3 English or Persian cucumbers
  • ¼ small red onion (optional), very thinly sliced

Seasoning & Draining

  • 1½–2 tsp kosher salt

Dressing

  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 2–3 tbsp lemon juice or rice vinegar
  • 1–3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • Salt & freshly cracked black pepper, to taste

Other

  • 4–7 oz (120–200g) feta cheese, broken or roughly smashed
  • Fresh dill or fresh herbs (optional)
  • Optional: red wine vinegar, a touch of honey

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Slice cucumbers into 3–4 inch sections, smash each flat with the side of a knife or your palm until cracks form. Tear or chop into bite-sized pieces. Place in a colander, sprinkle with salt, drain 15–30 minutes.
  2. Grate garlic into a bowl, stir in lemon juice (or rice vinegar), let sit ~10 minutes to mellow. Whisk in olive oil, season with salt and pepper.
  3. Pat drained cucumber pieces dry gently. Toss with dressing. Add feta chunks/crumbles and gently mix. Add red onion and dill if using. Adjust seasoning.
  4. Serve immediately, or refrigerate a few hours — toss again before serving.

💡 Tips

Smashing makes cucumbers juicier and more flavorful by breaking up the cells so dressing penetrates. If the salad gets watery, drain and press lightly again before serving.
