Crispy Air Fryer Apple Fries

A fun, crispy treat, healthier and perfect for snacking or dessert.

Ingredients

  • 2 large apples (Granny Smith or Honeycrisp)
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • Cooking spray

Dipping Sauce (optional)

  • ½ cup caramel sauce
  • ½ cup vanilla yogurt

Instructions

  1. Peel and slice apples into wedges, remove core and seeds.
  2. Mix cinnamon and sugar in a small bowl.
  3. Dredge apple wedges in flour, then dip in eggs, then coat with breadcrumb mixture.
  4. Spray air fryer basket with cooking spray, arrange fries.
  5. Air fry at 375°F for 10–12 minutes, flipping halfway.
  6. Serve with caramel sauce or vanilla yogurt.

Nutrition (per serving, ~6 servings)

~150 cal, 4g protein, 28g carbs, 2.6g fat.
