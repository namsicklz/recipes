Strawberry Grape Jelly

No-gelatin-packet jelly mold with fresh fruit set right in, bright lemon-sweetened base.

Yield one 9x9 panPrep ~10 minutesSet 4-6 hours (fridge)

🧂 Ingredients

  • 1-2 cups strawberries, sliced
  • 1-2 cups grapes
  • 1 cup hot water
  • 2 lemons, freshly squeezed
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 4 tbsp unflavoured gelatin

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Arrange sliced strawberries and grapes in desired mold (a 9x9 baking pan works well).
  2. In a heat-safe bowl, combine 1 cup hot water, lemon juice, and maple syrup.
  3. Add the 4 tbsp unflavoured gelatin to the hot liquid and mix until completely dissolved.
  4. Pour over the fruit.
  5. Set in the fridge for 4-6 hours.

🧊 Storage

Keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.
