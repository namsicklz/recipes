Crispy Orange Chicken Tacos

Orange chicken, smashed into a taco.

🧂 Ingredients (makes ~8 tacos)

Tacos

  • Flour tortillas
  • 1 lb ground chicken
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Beef tallow or avocado oil, for frying

Orange Glaze

  • 1 cup fresh squeezed orange juice
  • 2 tbsp orange or apricot marmalade
  • 1 1/2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp freshly grated ginger
  • 1 tsp freshly grated garlic
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp cold water

Slaw

  • 1 cup thinly sliced green cabbage
  • 1 cup thinly sliced red cabbage
  • 1 carrot, thinly sliced or shredded
  • 1/2 cup sliced green onions
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Sesame seeds
  • Salt, to taste

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Press a thin layer of ground chicken onto each tortilla; season with salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper.
  2. Press the chicken side into the seasoned panko until fully coated.
  3. Heat beef tallow or avocado oil and fry the tacos chicken-side down until golden and crispy. Flip and continue cooking until the chicken is cooked through.
  4. For the glaze: add the orange juice, marmalade, ketchup, soy sauce, ginger, and garlic to a pan over medium-low heat. Mix the cornstarch and cold water separately to make a slurry, then slowly stir it into the glaze. Simmer until thick and glossy.
  5. Combine all the slaw ingredients in a bowl and mix until coated.
  6. Dunk the crispy chicken side of each taco into the orange glaze, then top with the fresh slaw, sesame seeds, and extra green onions.

📊 Nutrition (per taco, ~8 tacos)

~400 cal, 19g protein, 44g carbs, 16g fat (rough estimate).
