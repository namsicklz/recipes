Chicken Gyros with Flatbread & Tzatziki

Ingredients (serves 4)

Chicken Marinade

  • 600g boneless chicken thighs
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tbsp lemon zest
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp yoghurt

Flatbread

  • 2 cups plain flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp yoghurt
  • ½ cup cold water (add gradually until soft dough forms)

Assembly

  • Tzatziki sauce
  • 1 bowl frozen steak-cut fries (air fried or baked)
  • ½ red onion, sliced
  • 1 chopped tomato
  • Fresh coriander or mint leaves

Instructions

  1. Mix marinade ingredients, coat chicken thighs, refrigerate 2–4 hours (overnight best).
  2. Grill, bake, or pan-fry chicken until golden and cooked through. Slice thinly.
  3. Mix flour, salt, yoghurt, and water into a soft dough. Rest 30 min, roll into rounds, cook on a hot skillet until puffed and golden.
  4. Bake or air fry fries; slice onions, chop tomatoes, prep herbs.
  5. Assemble: flatbread, chicken, fries, onions, tomatoes, tzatziki, herbs. Fold and serve warm.

Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~560 cal, 32g protein, 52g carbs, 22g fat.
