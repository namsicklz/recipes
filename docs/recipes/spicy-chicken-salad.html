Spicy Chicken Salad

🧂 Ingredients (serves ~3–4)

Chicken

  • 300g boneless chicken breast, cubed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ½ cup water

Dressing

  • ⅓ cup low-fat Greek yogurt
  • ¼ cup low-fat mayo
  • ¼ cup sriracha sauce
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • 1 tbsp honey

Salad Bowl

  • 2 cups shredded lettuce
  • ½ cup chopped tomato
  • ½ cup chopped onions
  • ½ cup chopped cucumber
  • ⅓ cup sweet corn

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. In a pan, combine chicken with oregano, salt, paprika, chilli flakes, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, and water. Cook over medium-high heat until water evaporates and chicken is cooked through with light charring.
  2. Whisk yogurt, mayo, sriracha, lime juice, and honey until smooth for the dressing.
  3. Layer lettuce, tomato, onion, cucumber, and sweet corn in a bowl. Add cooked chicken. Drizzle with dressing, toss lightly.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~310 cal, 27g protein, 15g carbs, 16g fat.
