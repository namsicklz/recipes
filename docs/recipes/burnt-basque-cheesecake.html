Burnt Basque Cheesecake

Includes a lemon blueberry topping variant.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 1kg cream cheese
  • 320g sugar
  • 6 large eggs
  • 30g fresh lemon juice
  • 15g vanilla extract
  • 5g sea salt
  • 600g heavy whipping cream
  • 30g all purpose flour

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Mix room temperature cream cheese with sugar until well blended. Mix in eggs one by one.
  2. Add lemon juice, salt, vanilla extract, and flour; mix well. Then add the cream and fold in — don't use a blender, use a spoon or spatula.
  3. Pour the batter into a 9x4 cake pan lined with parchment paper.
  4. Preheat oven to 450°F. Bake until the top is burnt.
  5. Cool at room temperature for 2 hours, then refrigerate overnight.
