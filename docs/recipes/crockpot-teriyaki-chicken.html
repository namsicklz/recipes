Crockpot Teriyaki Chicken

Source: social recipe share (Crockpot Series Episode 4). Sweet, savory, tender teriyaki chicken thighs, slow-cooked with onion and served over rice.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons butter, optional for searing
  • 1 cup shoyu
  • ¼ cup water
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon ground ginger
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 3 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1 tablespoon gochugaru, optional
  • 1 teaspoon honey, optional
  • broccoli, optional
  • steamed rice, for serving
  • green onions and sesame seeds, garnish

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Pat the chicken thighs dry then lightly season with SPG for extra color and flavor. Heat the butter in a skillet and sear the chicken for 2-3 minutes per side. The chicken does not need to be fully cooked.
  2. Place the sliced onion in the bottom of the crockpot and arrange the chicken thighs on top.
  3. Whisk together the shoyu, water, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, rice vinegar, cornstarch, and gochugaru and honey if using, then pour the sauce over the chicken.
  4. Cover and cook on LOW for 4½ hours or HIGH for 2-2½ hours, until the chicken is tender and reaches at least 185°F.
  5. During the final 30 minutes, add broccoli if using.
  6. Serve the chicken over steamed rice, spoon the teriyaki sauce over everything, and finish with green onions and optional sesame seeds.
