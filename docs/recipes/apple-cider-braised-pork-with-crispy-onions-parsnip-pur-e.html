Apple Cider Braised Pork with Crispy Onions & Parsnip Purée

Serves 4–6Prep 25 minCook 2.5 hours

🧂 Ingredients

Braised Pork

  • 3 lbs boneless pork shoulder
  • 1½ tbsp neutral oil (canola or grapeseed)
  • ⅔ gallon apple cider (~2.5 liters)
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp whole black peppercorns
  • 4 bay leaves
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 star anise
  • 1 tbsp molasses
  • 1 tbsp salt

Crispy Onions

  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ¼ tsp white pepper
  • ⅛ tsp MSG (optional)

Parsnip Purée

  • 1 lb parsnips, peeled and cut into chunks
  • Heavy cream, enough to just cover
  • Salt to taste

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C). Season pork generously with salt and pepper. Sear in oil in a large Dutch oven until deeply browned on all sides; remove and discard excess fat.
  2. Return pork to pot, add cider, stock, soy sauce, and aromatics (peppercorns, bay leaves, cinnamon, star anise, molasses, salt). Bring to a high simmer, cover tightly, transfer to oven. Braise 2½ hours until pork pulls apart easily.
  3. Soak sliced onions in buttermilk at least 30 minutes. Combine flour, salt, garlic powder, onion powder, white pepper, paprika, MSG in a shallow dish. Heat oil to 350°F (175°C), dredge onions, fry until golden and crisp (1–2 min). Drain and season lightly with salt.
  4. Cover parsnips with heavy cream in a saucepan, simmer until tender and cream thickens (~15 min). Blend smooth, season to taste.
  5. Remove pork from oven. Strain half the braising liquid, simmer until reduced and glossy (10–15 min). Shred or slice pork, spoon reduced sauce over top.
  6. Plate: parsnip purée, pulled pork, cider reduction drizzle, crispy onions on top.

💡 Chef Tips

A few apple slices or a touch of Dijon in the reduction complement the cider sweetness. Parsnip purée can be swapped for mashed sweet potatoes or celery root.
