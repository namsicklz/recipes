Perfect Pan-Seared Scallops (Butter, Lemon, Herbs)

Time 10 minutesServes 2

🧂 Ingredients

  • 8–10 large dry sea scallops
  • Kosher salt
  • Fresh cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil (avocado, grapeseed, or canola)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 clove garlic, lightly smashed
  • Zest of ½ lemon
  • Squeeze of fresh lemon juice
  • Fresh herbs (thyme, parsley, or chives)

The Key Rule

Dry scallops = good sear. Pat them very dry with paper towels — moisture is the enemy.

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Season scallops generously with salt and pepper right before cooking.
  2. Heat a heavy skillet over high heat, add oil until shimmering.
  3. Place scallops flat-side down. Do not move them. Cook 1½–2 minutes until a deep golden crust forms.
  4. Flip scallops. Add butter, garlic, and lemon zest. Tilt pan and spoon butter over scallops 30–45 seconds.
  5. Remove from heat, add a squeeze of lemon juice and herbs.

🍽️ Serving

Over mashed potatoes or cauliflower purée, with crusty bread, or alongside a simple arugula salad.

💡 Chef Tips

If scallops stick, they're not ready yet — wait 10 more seconds. Don't overcrowd the pan. Scallops should be just opaque in the center, not rubbery.
