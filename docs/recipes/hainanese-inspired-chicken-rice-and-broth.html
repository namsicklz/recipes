Hainanese-Inspired Chicken Rice and Broth

🧂 Ingredients (serves 1)

Chicken

  • 1 chicken breast, cut into two pieces
  • 2 bird's eye chilies, finely chopped (deseeded if preferred)
  • 5cm piece ginger, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  • 1 tsp white sugar
  • 1 tsp rice wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp chicken stock
  • Splash of neutral oil for frying

Rice

  • 5cm piece ginger, grated
  • 2 spring onions (white part only), finely chopped
  • 1 cup washed jasmine rice
  • 1.5 cups chicken stock
  • Neutral oil

Broth

  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 spring onions (green parts only)
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil

Lime Chili Sauce

  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 bird's eye chili, finely chopped
  • ¼ cup neutral oil
  • 1.5 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Juice of 1 lime

To Serve

  • 1 bok choy, cooked in leftover chicken stock
  • 1 spring onion, sliced
  • 1 cucumber, sliced

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Broth: Add chicken broth, soy sauce, spring onion greens, sesame oil to a pot. Simmer with lid on.
  2. Rice: Sauté ginger and spring onion whites in oil 1–2 min. Add rinsed rice and sesame oil, mix. Add chicken stock, cook using absorption method until fluffy.
  3. Chicken: Mix marinade (chili, ginger, garlic, sriracha, sugar, vinegar, sesame oil, chicken stock). Coat chicken. Fry in neutral oil until charred both sides; add leftover marinade and cook through. Remove. In the same pan, cook bok choy in remaining stock.
  4. Chili lime sauce: Heat neutral oil, pour carefully over garlic and chili. Stir in sesame oil, soy sauce, lime juice.
  5. Assemble: Rice in bowl, broth poured around, chicken and bok choy on top, garnish with spring onion and cucumber, spoon chili lime sauce over.
