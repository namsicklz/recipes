Coda alla Vaccinara (Roman Oxtail Stew)

Works with either traditional oxtail or beef stew meat (see substitution note).

Prep: 20 min · Cook: 3–3½ hours · Serves: 4–6

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs (1.4kg) oxtail, cut into 2–3 inch pieces (or beef stew meat, see note)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 oz (115g) pancetta or guanciale, diced
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped (plus 1 extra for garnish)
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup dry red wine
  • 28 oz (800g) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 sprig rosemary
  • 2 whole cloves (optional)
  • Salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp cocoa powder (or 1 square dark chocolate) — traditional Roman secret
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Pat meat dry, season with salt and pepper. Brown on all sides in olive oil in a large Dutch oven, working in batches. Remove and set aside.
  2. Lower heat to medium, add pancetta, cook until fat renders. Add onion, carrot, celery; sauté ~8 minutes. Stir in garlic and tomato paste, cook 1–2 minutes.
  3. Pour in red wine, scraping up browned bits. Simmer until reduced by half, ~5 minutes. Add crushed tomatoes, bay leaf, rosemary, cloves, and browned meat.
  4. Bring to a simmer, cover, cook on low heat 3 hours, stirring occasionally, until meat is tender and falling apart.
  5. Uncover, stir in cocoa powder or dark chocolate, simmer uncovered 15–20 minutes until thickened and glossy. Adjust seasoning.
  6. Serve warm with polenta, rigatoni, or mashed potatoes, garnished with parsley and sautéed celery.

Using Stew Meat Instead of Oxtail

Works fine — expect a slightly less gelatinous sauce since oxtail bones/collagen naturally thicken the stew. Use boneless beef chuck or short ribs, 2-inch cubes. Add ½ cup beef stock to replace richness. Simmer 2½ hours instead of 3–3½. For oxtail-like texture, stir in 1 tsp unflavored gelatin dissolved in warm stock near the end.

Nutrition (per serving)

~460 cal, 38g protein, 28g fat, 10g carbs, 6g sugar, 2g fiber.
