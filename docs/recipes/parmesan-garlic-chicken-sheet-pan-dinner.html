Parmesan Garlic Chicken Sheet Pan Dinner

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless chicken thighs
  • Buffalo Wild Wings Parmesan Garlic sauce
  • 1 tsp each: salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, onion powder
  • 2 tsp each: garlic powder, smoked paprika
  • Small head of broccoli, chopped
  • 24 oz baby potatoes
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • Olive oil
  • Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Spray a large sheet pan with cooking spray.
  2. Wash and quarter baby potatoes, slice onion, chop broccoli (leave pieces slightly larger).
  3. Add veggies to sheet pan, drizzle with olive oil, season with about half the seasoning blend.
  4. Season chicken thighs with remaining seasoning, coat with enough Parmesan Garlic sauce to cover.
  5. Bake at 400°F for ~35 minutes, or until chicken reaches internal temp of at least 185°F. Broil briefly if desired.
  6. Top with shredded Parmesan cheese. Enjoy.
