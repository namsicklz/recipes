Fried Shrimp & Mango Salsa Hand Rolls

Adapted from a Tasty recipe.

🧂 Ingredients (serves 3)

Mango Salsa

  • 1 mango, cubed
  • ½ cup red onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, cubed
  • 1 avocado, cubed
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper, to taste
  • ¼ cup cilantro, chopped

Seasoned Rice

  • 4 cups cooked rice
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp salt

Fried Shrimp Rolls

  • 1 lb shrimp, breaded with flour, egg, and panko, then fried (or air-fried)
  • Nori sheets (seaweed)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Combine mango, red onion, bell pepper, avocado, lime juice, olive oil, cilantro, salt & pepper for the salsa.
  2. Stir rice vinegar, sugar, and salt into warm cooked rice; let cool.
  3. Bread and fry shrimp until crispy.
  4. Place nori sheet, add rice, top with fried shrimp and mango salsa. Roll and serve immediately.

📊 Nutrition (per roll, ~3 rolls total)

~260 cal, 10g protein, 35g carbs, 8g fat.
