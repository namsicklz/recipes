Sausage & Mash Tacos

Reconstructed recipe — the original was only ever saved as a generated PDF that's no longer recoverable. This is a plausible version based on the name — a British-inspired mashup of bangers and mash served taco-style. Makes 4 servings (8 tacos).

🧂 Ingredients

  • 4 pork sausages, casings removed
  • 1½ lbs potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • ¼ cup milk or cream
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup beef or onion gravy
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • Chopped chives, for serving

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until fork-tender, about 15–20 minutes. Drain, then mash with butter, milk, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  2. Meanwhile, crumble the sausage meat into a skillet over medium heat. Cook, breaking it up, until browned and crisp in places, about 8 minutes. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same skillet, sauté the sliced onion until soft and caramelized, about 8–10 minutes.
  4. Warm the gravy in a small saucepan.
  5. Warm the tortillas. Spread a layer of mash on each, top with sausage, caramelized onion, and a drizzle of gravy.
  6. Finish with chopped chives and serve immediately.

📝 Notes

Essentially bangers and mash reassembled as a handheld — a fun way to use up leftover mash and gravy.
