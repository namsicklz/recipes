Wonton Lasagna

Layered pork, cabbage, and wonton wrappers steamed in a soufflé cup.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs ground pork (or chicken)
  • ½ cup green onion
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp corn starch
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp 5 spice
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp MSG (optional)
  • ½ cup water
  • Wonton wraps
  • Chili oil
  • Napa cabbage
  • ¼ cup water

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, combine pork, corn starch, garlic, sesame oil, 5 spice, MSG, oyster sauce, green onion, and water. Mix well.
  2. In a 10–12 oz soufflé cup, layer pork, cabbage, wonton wrap, and chili oil. Repeat for 3 layers. Add water on the top layer — do not add chili oil to the top layer until it's cooked.
  3. In a pot, add an inch of water. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, place soufflé cup in the pot, cover, and cook 8–10 minutes.
  4. Add chili oil on top layer and enjoy.
