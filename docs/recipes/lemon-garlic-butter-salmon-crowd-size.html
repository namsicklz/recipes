Lemon Garlic Butter Salmon (Crowd-Size)

A whole side of salmon, built for feeding a crowd (originally sized for a 9-person Mother's Day dinner) — easier to serve than whole fish, more forgiving in the oven, and looks impressive on a platter.

Ingredients

  • 1 large salmon side (4.5–5.5 lbs) — feeds ~9 people with sides
  • 1 stick butter
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 lemons (1 sliced, 1 juiced)
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • Fresh dill or parsley
  • Olive oil
  • Salt + pepper
  • Optional: paprika and red pepper flakes

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 400°F.
  2. Put salmon on a parchment-lined sheet pan.
  3. Melt butter and mix with garlic, lemon juice, Dijon, honey, salt, pepper, and a little olive oil.
  4. Pour over salmon, top with lemon slices and herbs.
  5. Bake about 20–28 minutes depending on thickness.
  6. Broil 1–2 minutes at the end for color.

Serve With

Spanish roasted potatoes, asparagus or green beans, a simple salad, crusty bread.

Notes

The honey + Dijon + butter combo gives restaurant-quality flavor without much effort. If going bigger/fancier for the occasion, whole roasted branzino (4 whole fish, ~1.5–2 lbs each, stuffed with lemon/garlic/herbs, roasted over potatoes/tomatoes/olives) is a "wow" alternative — but salmon wins on cost, ease, and low stress for feeding a crowd.
