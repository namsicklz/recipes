Slow Cooker Beef Stew

Source: adapted from NYT Cooking. Spoon-tender beef with a deep, dark sauce from stout beer and maple syrup.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 3 large carrots, peeled, cut into 1½-inch chunks
  • 2 large parsnips, peeled, cut into 1½-inch chunks
  • 2 large russet potatoes, peeled, cut into 1½-inch chunks
  • 3 thyme sprigs (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 2 rosemary sprigs (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 3–3½ lbs chuck roast, excess fat trimmed, cut into 2-inch chunks
  • ⅓ cup stout beer (or beef broth)
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • 4 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 3 tsp balsamic vinegar, preferably aged
  • Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Combine carrots, parsnips, potatoes, herbs, beef, beer, maple syrup, garlic, onion powder, garlic powder, and 1 tsp balsamic vinegar in a 5–8 quart slow cooker. Season with 2 tsp salt and generous pepper. Mix well.
  2. Cook on low for 10–12 hours until meat and vegetables are very tender. Stir once during cooking if possible.
  3. Remove and discard herb sprigs. Stir in remaining 2 tsp balsamic vinegar. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  4. Serve in shallow bowls with meat, vegetables, and sauce.

Optional — browning for extra flavor

Before slow cooking, season beef with salt and sear in 2 tbsp vegetable oil over high heat in batches. Deglaze pan with water or broth, scrape up browned bits, add to slow cooker. Can also be made stovetop/oven: sear meat, add rest of ingredients, simmer covered on low (or 325°F oven) for 2–3 hours until tender.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, 6 servings)

~540 cal, 45g protein, 26g carbs, 28g fat, 4g fiber, ~620mg sodium.
