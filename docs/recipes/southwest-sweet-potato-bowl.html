Southwest Sweet Potato Bowl

Ingredients

  • 1 large sweet potato (seasoned with tequila lime and a flaming hot seasoning)
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • ¼ cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 10 oz boneless skinless chicken thigh
  • ½ avocado
  • 4 tbsp charred sweet corn
  • 1 tbsp pickled red onion
  • 2 tbsp cotija cheese
  • Sliced jalapeno
  • ½ cup pico
  • Cilantro
  • Lime wedges

Instructions

  1. Poke holes in sweet potato, wrap in wet paper towel, microwave 8 minutes (or cook however you'd like).
  2. Coat chicken in avocado oil and seasoning, air fry thighs at 400°F for 15–20 minutes.
  3. Layer sweet potato, yogurt, chicken, avocado, corn, pickled onion, cotija, jalapeno, pico, cilantro, and lime wedges. Serve.

Health notes

High protein, rich in fiber, heart-healthy fats from avocado, blood-sugar friendly when paired with protein and healthy fats.
