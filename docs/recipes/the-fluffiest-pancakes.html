The Fluffiest Pancakes You'll Ever Make

Source: Natasha's Baking. Makes 6 pancakes, 5.5" (14cm).

🧂 Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 160g sugar
  • 20g baking powder
  • 20g avocado oil
  • 8g salt
  • 400g cake flour
  • 400g milk

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Whisk together all ingredients in a large bowl until a smooth, lump-free batter forms.
  2. Heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over low heat, spray with avocado oil.
  3. Pour batter into the pan (~⅓ cup per pancake).
  4. Cook on low until bubbles form and cover the surface.
  5. Flip gently, cook until golden brown and cooked through.
  6. Serve warm with toppings, or store in a ziplock bag in the fridge for later.

📊 Nutrition (per pancake, approx.)

270 cal, 6g fat, 44g carbs, 8g protein, 14g sugar, 220mg sodium.
