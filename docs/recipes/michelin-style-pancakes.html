Michelin-Style Pancakes at Home

Soufflé-style Japanese-technique pancakes, steamed and finished with maple butter.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 240g egg whites (from 8 large eggs)
  • 20g lemon juice
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 20g corn flour
  • 100g egg yolks (from 5 large eggs)
  • 140g 00 flour
  • 4g salt
  • 50g whole milk

Batter

  1. Whisk egg whites and lemon juice to 60% peaks.
  2. Gradually add sugar, whisk to 80% peaks.
  3. Whisk in corn flour to combine.
  4. Add egg yolks, whisk to combine.
  5. Very gently fold in flour and salt.
  6. Finally, fold in the milk.

Cooking

  1. Heat a small non-stick egg pan on low. Melt a knob of butter, wipe pan with kitchen paper to remove excess.
  2. Ladle in 200g of batter (about 3 ladles) and cover.
  3. Cook ~5 minutes until edges come away from the sides and self-support.
  4. Gently flip (underside should be golden), cook the other side ~1 minute — no need to color it as much, or the pancake will dry out.
  5. Wrap loosely in parchment paper, ready to steam.

Finishing

  1. Place the pancake, still wrapped in parchment, into a steamer and cover for 4 minutes.
  2. Carefully unwrap, place on a rack, pipe or spoon over maple butter.
  3. Serve with a disc of salted butter.
