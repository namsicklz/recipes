Toasted Orzo Potato Leek Soup

Source: Brita Cooks

Ingredients

  • 1 leek (white + light green parts), chopped
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 potato, cubed
  • 4 strips bacon, chopped
  • 1 cup orzo
  • ~4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2 cups water
  • Toppings: crème fraîche or sour cream, crispy bacon, fresh chives, salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook chopped bacon in a large pot over medium heat until crispy. Remove bacon, leaving ~1 tbsp fat in the pot.
  2. Add dry orzo to the bacon fat, toast over medium heat until golden brown (3–4 min). Remove, set aside.
  3. Melt butter in the same pot. Sauté leek, shallot, and garlic ~5 minutes until softened and fragrant.
  4. Pour in chicken stock + water, add cubed potato and bay leaf. Simmer ~5 minutes.
  5. Return toasted orzo to the pot, cook ~10 minutes until orzo and potatoes are tender. Remove bay leaf.
  6. Stir in frozen peas, simmer 2 more minutes. Season with salt & pepper.
  7. Serve topped with crème fraîche or sour cream, crispy bacon, and chives.
