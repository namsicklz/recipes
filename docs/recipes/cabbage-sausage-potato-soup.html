Cabbage, Sausage & Potato Soup

Source: gimmesomeoven.com

Ingredients (serves 8)

  • 1 lb kielbasa sausage, sliced into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 medium leeks, sliced (white and pale green parts only)
  • 2 medium carrots, peeled and diced
  • 1 stalk celery, diced
  • 1 small green cabbage, chopped into bite-sized pieces
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 lb red potatoes, diced
  • 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Kosher salt & freshly-cracked black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Brown sausage in a large stockpot over medium heat, 5–6 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon, set aside.
  2. In the same pot, add olive oil, leeks, carrots, celery. Sauté 5 minutes. Stir in cabbage and garlic, cook 4 more minutes.
  3. Add stock, diced potatoes, Italian seasoning, bay leaf, and reserved sausage. Bring to a simmer, reduce heat, cover, cook ~15 minutes until potatoes are tender.
  4. Discard bay leaf, season with salt and pepper. Serve warm, optionally with crusty bread or fresh herbs.

Nutrition (per serving, ~8 servings)

~360 cal, 18g protein, 20g carbs, 22g fat.
