Crispy Smashed Sweet Potatoes with Whipped Feta & Hot Honey

Serves: 4 · Prep: 15 min · Cook: 45 min

Ingredients

Sweet Potatoes

  • 4 small sweet potatoes
  • Olive oil, for drizzling
  • Salt & black pepper

Whipped Feta

  • 200g feta cheese
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt (or cream cheese)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Zest of ½ lemon

Toppings

  • Hot honey (store-bought, or mix ¼ cup honey + ½ tsp chili flakes)
  • Crushed roasted pistachios
  • Fresh thyme or parsley (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil sweet potatoes whole until just fork-tender, ~15–20 minutes. Drain, cool slightly.
  2. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  3. Place boiled potatoes on a parchment-lined tray, smash flat with the bottom of a glass.
  4. Drizzle generously with olive oil, season with salt and pepper. Roast 25–30 minutes until crispy and golden.
  5. Blend feta, Greek yogurt, lemon zest, and olive oil until smooth and creamy.
  6. Spread whipped feta on a serving plate, top with crispy smashed sweet potatoes.
  7. Drizzle with hot honey, finish with pistachios and herbs.
