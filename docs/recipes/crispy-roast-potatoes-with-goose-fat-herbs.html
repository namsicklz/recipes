Crispy Roast Potatoes with Goose Fat & Herbs

Ingredients (serves ~6)

  • 2 lbs Maris Piper potatoes (~900g)
  • 4 tbsp goose fat (or substitute duck fat)
  • 1 beef stock cube (or stock pot)
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • Salt & freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp Aromat (optional)
  • 3 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 2 sprigs thyme

Instructions

  1. Clean, peel, and quarter the potatoes.
  2. Place in a pot of cold water with the beef stock cube. Bring to a boil, simmer ~10 minutes until a knife pierces with some resistance.
  3. Drain, reserving the potato water for gravy. Rough up the potatoes in the pan, let cool 10 minutes.
  4. Transfer to a baking dish. Sprinkle with flour, salt, pepper, Aromat, and herbs.
  5. Heat goose fat until liquid, pour over potatoes evenly to coat.
  6. Roast at 180°C (350°F), turning carefully every 30 minutes (~1.5 hours total).
  7. Serve golden and crispy — use the potato water for gravy.

Nutrition (per serving, ~6 servings)

~260 kcal, 5g protein, 34g carbs, 12g fat.
