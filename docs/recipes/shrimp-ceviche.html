Shrimp Ceviche

Ingredients (serves ~4)

  • 1 lb (450g) raw shrimp, peeled & deveined, chopped into ½-inch pieces
  • 1 cup fresh lime juice (about 8–10 limes)
  • ½ cup fresh lemon juice (about 3–4 lemons)
  • 1 cup tomato, diced
  • ½ cup red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 cucumber, peeled & diced
  • 1 jalapeño (or serrano), minced (remove seeds for less heat)
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Salt & pepper, to taste
  • Optional: hot sauce, to serve
  • Tortilla chips or tostadas, for serving

Instructions

  1. Place chopped shrimp in a glass bowl, cover with lime and lemon juice. Stir, cover, refrigerate 30–45 minutes until shrimp turn opaque. (Or blanch shrimp 1 minute in boiling water first for food safety, then marinate.)
  2. While marinating, dice tomato, cucumber, onion, jalapeño, and avocado.
  3. Drain about half the citrus juice from the shrimp. Add veggies, cilantro, salt, pepper. Toss gently.
  4. Chill another 15 minutes to let flavors meld. Serve with tortilla chips or tostadas.

Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~230 cal, 24g protein, 12g carbs, 9g fat.
