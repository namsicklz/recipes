Quick Pickled Red Onions

Prep: 5 min · Total: 30 min (plus cooling) · Makes: 1 pint jar

Ingredients

  • 1 large red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup boiling water
  • ½ cup apple cider vinegar (or white vinegar for sharper flavor)
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • Optional: ½ tsp black peppercorns, 1 small smashed garlic clove, 2–3 sprigs fresh herbs (thyme, cilantro, dill), ¼ tsp red pepper flakes

Instructions

  1. Peel and thinly slice the red onion (mandoline helps for even slices).
  2. Combine boiling water, vinegar, sugar, and salt in a heatproof bowl; stir until dissolved.
  3. Pack sliced onions tightly into a clean glass jar, add any optional flavorings.
  4. Pour hot brine over the onions until fully submerged, pressing down with a spoon if needed.
  5. Let cool uncovered ~30 minutes at room temperature.
  6. Cover and refrigerate. Ready to eat in about 1 hour, better after a few hours.

Storage

Keeps 2–3 weeks refrigerated. Keep onions submerged in brine.
