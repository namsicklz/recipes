Beef & Broccoli Meal Prep

Source: Tasty. Makes 2 servings. Prep 10 min, cook 30 min, total 40 min. Cooked entirely in a rice cooker.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 1 cup jasmine rice, rinsed
  • 1 cup bone broth
  • 2 tbsp teriyaki sauce, divided
  • ½ lb ribeye steak, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup broccoli florets

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Add rice, 1 tbsp teriyaki sauce, and bone broth to the rice cooker bowl.
  2. In a small bowl, combine the steak with the remaining 1 tbsp teriyaki sauce.
  3. Layer the seasoned steak over the rice, then arrange broccoli florets on top.
  4. Cook using the regular quick-cook setting.
  5. Serve and enjoy.

📊 Nutrition (per serving)

502 cal, 35g protein, 41g carbs, 22g fat, 22g fiber, 4g sugar.
