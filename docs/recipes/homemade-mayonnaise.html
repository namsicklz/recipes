Homemade Mayonnaise (Classic & Creamy)

Ingredients

  • 1 large egg
  • 2 large egg yolks
  • 3 tsp white vinegar
  • Juice of 1 lemon (~2 tbsp)
  • 1 heaping tsp salt
  • 1 heaping tsp granulated sugar
  • ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  • Pinch cayenne pepper
  • 400ml (1¾ cups) avocado oil (can substitute neutral oil or light olive oil)

Instructions

  1. In a blender, tall jar (for immersion blender), or mixing bowl, combine whole egg + yolks, vinegar, lemon juice, salt, sugar, Dijon mustard, and cayenne.
  2. Blend on low speed 10–20 seconds until smooth.
  3. While blending, VERY slowly drizzle in the avocado oil in a thin stream. Mixture will emulsify and thicken gradually.
  4. If too thick, add 1–2 tsp water or lemon juice. If too thin, continue adding oil.
  5. Transfer to an airtight jar and refrigerate. Shelf life: 5–7 days.

Flavor Variations

  • Garlic Aioli: 2 grated garlic cloves + extra squeeze lemon
  • Chipotle Mayo: 1 tbsp adobo sauce + chipotle pepper
  • Herbed Mayo: 2 tbsp chopped parsley + chives
  • Truffle Mayo: 1 tsp truffle oil + cracked pepper
