Pasta e Fagioli

Thick, hearty Italian bean and pasta stew.

Total Time 45 minutes (using canned beans)Servings 4–6Calories ~320/serving (approx.)

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed (or 1 lb dry, soaked overnight)
  • 8 oz ditalini or small pasta
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 4 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 sprig fresh rosemary (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: 4 oz pancetta or bacon, diced, for extra flavor

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. If using dry beans, cook in water with bay leaves until tender (~1 hour), then drain and set aside.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  3. Add pancetta (if using), cook until crisp, ~5 minutes.
  4. Stir in onion, carrots, celery, and garlic; sauté until softened, 8–10 minutes.
  5. Add crushed tomatoes, broth, rosemary, bay leaves, salt, and pepper. Bring to a simmer.
  6. Mash half the beans lightly with a fork and add all beans to the pot.
  7. Simmer gently 20–25 minutes to let flavors meld.
  8. Cook pasta separately until al dente, drain, and stir into the soup just before serving.
  9. Remove bay leaves and rosemary sprig, adjust seasoning.

🔀 Variations

Nonna-style thicker version (more mashed beans), slow-cooker/Instant Pot version, or regional variations (Tuscan, Roman, Neapolitan).
