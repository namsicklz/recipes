Crispy Hot Honey Lemon Feta Chicken Cutlets

Reconstructed recipe — the original was only ever saved as a generated PDF (built by combining a few ingredients/photos in chat) that's no longer recoverable. This is a plausible version based on the name, not the original. Makes 4 servings.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, halved and pounded thin
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1½ cups panko breadcrumbs
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • Salt and pepper
  • Oil for frying

Hot Honey Lemon Glaze

  • ⅓ cup honey
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes (or hot sauce, to taste)
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • Pinch of salt

To finish

  • ½ cup crumbled feta
  • Fresh parsley or basil, chopped

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Set up a dredging station: flour in one bowl, beaten eggs in another, panko mixed with Parmesan and a pinch of salt/pepper in a third.
  2. Dredge each cutlet in flour, then egg, then press firmly into the panko mixture to coat.
  3. Heat ¼ inch of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry cutlets 3–4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Drain on a wire rack.
  4. Meanwhile, warm the honey in a small saucepan with red pepper flakes, lemon zest, lemon juice, and salt for 2–3 minutes until fragrant.
  5. Drizzle the hot honey lemon glaze over the crispy cutlets, then top with crumbled feta and fresh herbs.

📝 Notes

Serve immediately so the cutlets stay crisp under the glaze — hold the glaze on the side if prepping ahead.
