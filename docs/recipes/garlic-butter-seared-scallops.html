Garlic Butter Seared Scallops

Ingredients

  • 1.5–2 lbs large sea scallops (U-10 or U-12)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, cold
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Remove side muscle from scallops, pat completely dry. Let sit on paper towels 10–15 minutes. Season with salt and pepper just before cooking.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high to high heat for 3–4 minutes. Add oil, heat until shimmering.
  3. Sear scallops without moving for 2–3 minutes until golden brown. Flip, cook 1–2 minutes more until just done.
  4. Remove scallops, reduce heat to medium-low. Add garlic, cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. Add cold butter, swirl to melt. Add lemon juice and parsley, swirl to combine.
  6. Return scallops to pan, spoon sauce over. Serve immediately while hot.

Notes

Don't overcrowd the pan — cook in batches if needed. Scallops should sizzle aggressively when they hit the hot oil. Don't move them until they release naturally.
