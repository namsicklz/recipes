5-Layer Brownie Cookie Cheesecake

Layers (bottom to top): brownie base, cookie dough, cheesecake, chocolate ganache, whipped cream/toppings.

Serves: 12

🧂 Ingredients

Brownie Base

  • 1 box brownie mix (or homemade, ~18 oz)
  • Eggs, oil, and water (per package instructions)

Cookie Dough Layer

  • 1 cup flour (heat-treated for safety)
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup white sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • ½ cup mini chocolate chips

Cheesecake Layer

  • 16 oz (450g) cream cheese, softened
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ cup sour cream

Chocolate Ganache

  • 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • ½ cup heavy cream

Toppings

  • Whipped cream, crushed cookies or brownie chunks, chocolate drizzle

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Prepare brownie batter as directed. Bake in a greased springform pan at 350°F (175°C) for ~20 minutes (slightly underbaked).
  2. Mix softened butter, sugars, vanilla, and milk until creamy. Stir in flour and chocolate chips. Spread gently over cooled brownie base.
  3. Beat cream cheese, sugar, eggs, vanilla, sour cream until smooth. Pour over cookie dough layer. Bake at 325°F (160°C) for 40–50 minutes, until set but slightly jiggly in center. Chill completely (at least 4 hrs, preferably overnight).
  4. Heat cream, pour over chocolate chips, stir until smooth. Spread over cheesecake, chill 15 minutes.
  5. Top with whipped cream, crushed cookies, brownie bits, or extra chocolate drizzle.

📊 Nutrition (per slice, ~12 servings)

~585 cal, 7g protein, 55g carbs, 37g fat.
