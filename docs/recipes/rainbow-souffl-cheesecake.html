Rainbow Soufflé Cheesecake

Bakes up like a cross between a Japanese cotton cheesecake and a classic soufflé cheesecake — light, airy, with rainbow sprinkles.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz cream cheese (room temp)
  • 5 egg yolks, 5 egg whites
  • ¼ cup butter
  • ½ cup milk
  • ¼ cup flour + 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • Rainbow sprinkles

Instructions

  1. In a medium saucepan over medium heat, combine milk, butter, and cream cheese until smooth. Turn off heat, mix in flour and egg yolks.
  2. In a large bowl, whip egg whites and sugar until stiff peaks form.
  3. Fold in both mixes, add sprinkles, pour into a 7-inch springform pan lined tall with parchment paper (allows the cheesecake to grow like a soufflé).
  4. Place a pan with water on the bottom oven rack. Place cheesecake on the middle rack.
  5. Bake at 350°F for 20 minutes, then lower to 300°F and bake an extra 40 minutes.
  6. Let cool at room temperature before slicing. Store in the fridge.
