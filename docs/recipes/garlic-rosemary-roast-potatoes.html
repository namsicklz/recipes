Garlic & Rosemary Roast Potatoes

Christmas countdown roastie recipe.

Ingredients (serves ~6)

  • 2 lbs potatoes (~900g), peeled and sliced
  • 1 beef stock cube (or stock pot)
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 4 tbsp goose fat (or duck fat)
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • 6 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
  • Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel and slice potatoes. Place in a saucepan with beef stock, cover with cold water. Bring to a boil, simmer 4 minutes until just starting to soften.
  2. Drain in a colander, rough up edges, sprinkle with flour, leave to steam 5 minutes.
  3. Place goose fat in a roasting tin, heat in oven at 190°C (375°F) until smoking hot.
  4. Add potatoes, turn to coat, scatter in rosemary. Roast 50–60 minutes, turning every 10 minutes.
  5. Add garlic cloves after 15 minutes, continue roasting until golden and crisp.
  6. Season with salt and pepper before serving.

Nutrition (per serving, ~6 servings)

~270 kcal, 5g protein, 36g carbs, 12g fat.
