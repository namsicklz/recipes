Chicken Noodle Stir Fry

Ingredients (serves 4)

Stir Fry Base

  • ⅓ cup oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • ⅓ cup chopped green onions
  • 400g boneless chicken, sliced thin
  • ½ tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 medium carrot, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • Handful bean sprouts (optional)

Noodles

  • 300g medium egg noodles (cooked per packet)

Sauce

  • ¼ cup hot sriracha sauce
  • 2–3 tbsp honey
  • ⅓ cup soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • Handful chopped green onions

Instructions

  1. Boil noodles per packet instructions; drain and rinse with cold water to prevent sticking.
  2. Heat oil in a wok over high heat. Stir-fry garlic and green onions until fragrant. Add chicken, salt, chilli powder, pepper, chilli flakes; cook until browned.
  3. Add onion, carrot, bell pepper, bean sprouts; stir-fry 2–3 minutes until just tender.
  4. Stir in sriracha, honey, soy sauce.
  5. Add noodles, toss quickly over high heat until coated but not overcooked.
  6. Sprinkle sesame seeds, garnish with green onions. Serve hot.

Notes

Boil noodles while stir-frying to save time. Don't cook on low heat or sauté noodles too long — they'll dry out.

Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~520 cal, 29g protein, 60g carbs, 18g fat.
