Garlic Sweet Chilli Baked Chicken

Source: @shadi_faleel

🧂 Ingredients (serves 3–4)

  • 3–4 chicken legs (180–200g each)
  • ½ tbsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp chopped red chillies
  • 1 tbsp grated garlic
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • ⅓ cup sweet chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp oil

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven or air fryer to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Mix chilli powder, salt, chopped chillies, garlic, lemon juice, sweet chilli sauce, and oil into a marinade.
  3. Coat chicken legs thoroughly; let sit at least 20 minutes (up to 2 hours for deeper flavor).
  4. Bake or air fry 25–30 minutes, flipping halfway, until cooked through and lightly caramelized.
  5. Serve hot with rice, salad, or roasted veggies.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~295 cal, 28g protein, 8g carbs, 16g fat.
