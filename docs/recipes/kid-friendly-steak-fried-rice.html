Kid-Friendly Steak Fried Rice

Oyster-sauce-free version, adapted to be milder for an 11-year-old.

🧂 Ingredients (serves 4)

Steak

  • 1 lb (450g) flank steak or sirloin, thinly sliced against the grain
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp hoisin sauce (sweet, mild substitute for oyster sauce)
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)
  • Pinch of black pepper

Fried Rice

  • 3 cups cold cooked jasmine rice (day-old is best)
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 1 small carrot, finely diced
  • ½ cup frozen peas
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp hoisin sauce
  • Salt to taste (go light)

Optional garnish (adults)

  • Chopped green onions, sesame seeds

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Mix soy sauce, hoisin, cornstarch, sesame oil, pepper. Marinate sliced steak at least 15 minutes.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet or wok. Cook steak over medium-high until browned, ~3 minutes. Remove, set aside.
  3. Add remaining oil, sauté onion, carrot, peas 3–4 minutes. Add garlic, stir 30 seconds.
  4. Push veggies aside, scramble eggs until just set, mix in.
  5. Add cooked rice, breaking up clumps. Stir-fry 3–4 minutes. Stir in soy sauce + hoisin. Add steak back, toss, cook 1–2 more minutes.
  6. Serve warm; adults can top with green onions/sesame seeds.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, 4 servings)

~480 cal, 31g protein, 50g carbs, 15g fat, 3g fiber, ~720mg sodium.
