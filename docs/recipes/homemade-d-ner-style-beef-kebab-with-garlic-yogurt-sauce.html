Homemade Döner-Style Beef Kebab with Garlic Yogurt Sauce

Baked as a slab between parchment paper, sliced thin, and crisped — no skewers or grill needed.

🧂 Ingredients (makes 4–6 servings)

Beef Mixture

  • 2 lbs (900g) ground beef, 80–85% lean
  • 1 small onion, grated, then squeezed to remove moisture (use mostly the juice + 1 tbsp pulp)
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated or minced
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika (sweet)
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp coriander (optional but authentic)
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ¼ tsp cayenne (optional)
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil or melted beef fat (optional, helps texture)

Garlic Yogurt Sauce

  • 1 cup plain full-fat Greek yogurt (or strained Turkish yogurt)
  • 1–2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice (or ½ tbsp white vinegar)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (optional but authentic)
  • ¼ tsp salt, more to taste
  • 1–2 tbsp cold water, to thin

👩‍🍳 Instructions

Kebab

  1. Grate onion into a bowl, squeeze through your hand or cheesecloth to extract juice; save the juice and 1 tbsp of pulp. Add grated garlic.
  2. Combine ground beef, onion juice/pulp, garlic, and all spices in a bowl. Mix very thoroughly (2–3 min) until sticky and cohesive — this gives the classic döner texture.
  3. Place a sheet of parchment on a baking tray, add the meat, cover with a second parchment sheet. Press or roll into a ¾-inch-thick (2cm) rectangle. Peel back top paper and lay it loosely back on.
  4. Heat oven to 325°F (165°C). Bake 35–45 minutes until fully cooked (155–160°F internal). Rest 10 minutes for cleaner slicing.
  5. Slice very thinly (⅛–¼ inch), against the grain.
  6. Crisp before serving: pan-fry in a little oil/butter, 1–2 minutes per side until browned and crispy, or broil 2–3 minutes until browned.

Garlic Yogurt Sauce

  1. Whisk yogurt, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, and salt. Add water little by little until smooth and drizzle-able.
  2. Adjust: more lemon for tang, more garlic for punch, more salt if needed. Refrigerate at least 15 minutes.

Serve With

Pita or lavash, shredded lettuce, tomato, red onion, pickled chilies, chili sauce.
