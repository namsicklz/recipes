Sweet Potato Wraps

Reconstructed recipe — the original was only ever saved as a generated PDF that's no longer recoverable. This is a plausible version based on the name, in the same style as Grilled Zucchini & Chickpea Wraps with Herbed Yogurt Sauce elsewhere in this collection. Makes 4 wraps.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into ½-inch cubes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp cumin, salt and pepper
  • 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage or lettuce
  • ½ cup crumbled feta or shredded cheese
  • ¼ cup cilantro, chopped

Yogurt-lime sauce

  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • Salt, to taste

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss the sweet potato cubes with olive oil, paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper. Roast on a sheet pan 25–30 minutes, until tender and caramelized, stirring halfway.
  2. Warm the black beans in a small pan, seasoning lightly with salt and cumin.
  3. Whisk together the yogurt sauce ingredients.
  4. Warm the tortillas. Layer each with roasted sweet potato, black beans, cabbage, cheese, and cilantro.
  5. Drizzle with the yogurt-lime sauce, fold in the sides, and roll up tightly.

📝 Notes

Good served warm or cold — holds up well for meal prep.
