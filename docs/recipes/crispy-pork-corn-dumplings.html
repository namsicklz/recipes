Crispy Pork & Corn Dumplings with Crispy Skirt

Reconstructed recipe — the original was only ever saved as a generated PDF that's no longer recoverable. This is a plausible version based on the name, not the original. Makes about 24 dumplings.

🧂 Ingredients

Filling

  • ¾ lb ground pork
  • ¾ cup corn kernels (fresh or thawed frozen)
  • 2 green onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp minced ginger
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp shaoxing wine (optional)
  • ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp white pepper

Assembly & Skirt

  • 24 round dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • ½ cup water mixed with 1 tsp flour (for the crispy skirt)

Dipping sauce

  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp chili oil

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Combine all filling ingredients in a bowl and mix well.
  2. Place a spoonful of filling in the center of each wrapper. Wet the edges, fold in half, and pleat to seal.
  3. Heat oil in a large nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Arrange dumplings flat-side down in a circle, not touching.
  4. Fry 1–2 minutes until the bottoms are golden.
  5. Pour in the flour-water mixture (enough to come about halfway up the dumplings), cover, and steam 5–6 minutes until the water evaporates and the filling is cooked through.
  6. Uncover and let the remaining liquid crisp into a lacy golden "skirt" connecting the dumplings, 2–3 minutes more.
  7. Slide out onto a plate skirt-side up. Serve with the dipping sauce.

📝 Notes

The "crispy skirt" is the thin lacy layer of fried batter that forms around the base of the dumplings once the steaming water reduces and fries off — a classic Chinese potsticker technique.
