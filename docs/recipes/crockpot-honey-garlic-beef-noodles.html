Crockpot Honey Garlic Beef & Noodles

Source: social recipe share (Crockpot Series Episode 12). Tender chuck roast in a sweet and savory honey garlic sauce, tossed with yakisoba noodles.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2½ pounds chuck roast
  • your favorite beef rub, to taste
  • 2 tablespoons butter, for searing
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup soy sauce or shoyu
  • ½ cup crushed garlic
  • 1 teaspoon white pepper
  • 2 tablespoons rice cooking wine
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • ½ cup beef broth
  • precooked yakisoba stir-fry noodles
  • sliced green onions
  • chili oil for heat, optional
  • sesame seeds, garnish

Cornstarch slurry:

  • 3 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 3 tablespoons cold water

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Pat the chuck roast dry and season with your favorite beef rub. Melt the butter in a skillet over medium high heat and sear the roast on all sides until browned.
  2. Whisk together the brown sugar, soy sauce, crushed garlic, white pepper, rice cooking wine, honey, and beef broth.
  3. Place the roast in the crockpot and pour the sauce over it. Cover and cook on LOW for 8-10 hours, or until fork-tender.
  4. Carefully remove the beef and cut it into bite-size cubes. Skim excess fat from the sauce.
  5. Mix the cornstarch and cold water until smooth. Switch the crockpot to HIGH, stir the slurry into the hot sauce, and cook for 10-15 minutes, or until thickened.
  6. Return the cubed beef to the sauce. Add your precooked yakisoba stir-fry noodles and green onions, gently tossing until coated and heated through.
  7. Serve right away! Don't let the noodles sit in the sauce too long - they'll keep soaking it up.
