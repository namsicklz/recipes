BLT Egg Salad Lettuce Wraps

Ingredients (serves ~4)

  • 8 large eggs, hard boiled and chopped
  • 4 slices cooked bacon, crumbled
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, diced
  • ¼ cup mayonnaise (or Greek yogurt for lighter version)
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt & pepper, to taste
  • Romaine or butter lettuce leaves, for wraps

Instructions

  1. Combine chopped eggs, bacon, and tomatoes in a mixing bowl.
  2. Stir in mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
  3. Spoon mixture into lettuce leaves to make wraps.
  4. Serve immediately, or chill up to 2 days for meal prep.

Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~265 cal, 14g protein, 3g carbs, 22g fat.
