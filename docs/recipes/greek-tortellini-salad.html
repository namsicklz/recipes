Greek Tortellini Salad

Source: The Slow Roasted Italian

🧂 Ingredients (serves 8)

  • 8 oz (½ of a 16–19 oz package) cheese tortellini, cooked per package instructions
  • 1 orange bell pepper, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 yellow bell pepper, cut into bite-size pieces
  • ½ red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 English cucumber, cut into spears then quartered
  • 16 oz cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ cup sliced Kalamata olives (plus a bit more finely minced for dressing)
  • 4 oz feta cheese, crumbled

Greek Dressing

  • ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • ¼ cup red wine vinegar
  • ½ tsp dried oregano (crushed by hand for more flavor)
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp granulated sugar

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Boil tortellini per package directions (don't salt the water). Drain and let cool.
  2. Chop bell peppers, red onion, cucumber, tomatoes, olives, feta; combine with cooled tortellini in a large bowl.
  3. In a jar, combine dressing ingredients plus a bit of finely minced Kalamata olive. Shake well.
  4. Pour dressing over the salad, toss until coated. Serve immediately or chill to let flavors meld — layer with dressing on the bottom if transporting.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, 8 servings)

228 cal, 8g protein, 20g carbs, 14g fat.
