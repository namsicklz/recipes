Classic Cajun Seasoning

Bold, smoky, spicy, balanced. Great for chicken, shrimp, steak, roasted veggies, mac & cheese.

Makes: ~5 tablespoons

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp paprika (smoked for a deeper flavor)
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp onion powder
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp dried thyme
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt (reduce to 1–2 tsp for low-salt)
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust heat: ½ tsp mild / 2 tsp spicy)
  • 1 tsp white pepper (optional but authentic)
  • 1 tsp ground mustard (optional, adds tang)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

Whisk together and store in an airtight container for up to 6 months.

🔀 Variations

  • Extra Smoky: swap half the paprika for smoked paprika, add ½ tsp chipotle powder.
  • Sweet Heat: add 1 tsp brown sugar and 1 tsp chili powder.
  • Low-Sodium: reduce salt to 1 tsp, add ½ tsp celery seed for savory depth.

What to Use It On

Grilled chicken/shrimp, Cajun pasta, blackened salmon, roasted potatoes, Cajun butter for steak, mac & cheese topping (toasted).
