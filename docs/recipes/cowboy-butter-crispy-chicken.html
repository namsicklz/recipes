Cowboy Butter Crispy Chicken

Credit: @donaldo_cooks

🧂 Ingredients (serves ~4)

Cowboy Butter (Light Version)

  • 1 tbsp fresh garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh chives, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme, chopped
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • Salt & black pepper, to taste
  • 6 tbsp light mayonnaise

Chicken

  • 4 chicken thighs (boneless, skinless)
  • Cowboy butter marinade (reserve some for serving)

Cucumber Salad

  • 1 whole cucumber, thinly sliced
  • ¼ small cabbage, shredded
  • 1 jalapeño, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp dill
  • 1 tbsp pink Himalayan salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tbsp parsley
  • 1 tbsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp light mayonnaise

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Mix all cowboy butter ingredients until smooth; set some aside for spreading on bread/sandwiches.
  2. Coat chicken thighs in cowboy butter; refrigerate at least 1 hour (overnight best).
  3. Air fry at 385°F (195°C) for 16 minutes, flipping halfway, until internal temp reaches 165°F (74°C).
  4. Combine cucumber salad ingredients in a container; shake/mix well, chill before serving.
  5. Plate crispy chicken with cucumber salad; optionally serve with bread and reserved cowboy butter.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~390 cal, 34g protein, 9g carbs, 24g fat.
