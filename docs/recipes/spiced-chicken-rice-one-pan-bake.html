Spiced Chicken & Rice (One-Pan Bake)

Originally made with coconut milk; built-in lighter (no-coconut) option below.

🧂 Ingredients (serves 4–5)

Chicken

  • 1½ lbs chicken thighs (4–5 pieces)
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp tomato chicken bouillon
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Leaves from 3 sprigs thyme
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Rice

  • 1½ cups long-grain parboiled rice, rinsed
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 3 sprigs thyme (whole)
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • ½ tbsp tomato chicken bouillon
  • 1½ tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt, to taste

Choose ONE liquid option:

  • Option A (creamy, no coconut): 2½ cups chicken stock + ½ cup heavy cream OR evaporated milk
  • Option B (lighter): 3 cups chicken stock + 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • Original: 2 cups coconut milk + 1 cup chicken stock

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Mix cayenne, garlic powder, smoked paprika, cumin, chili powder, tomato bouillon, salt, and thyme leaves with olive oil into a paste. Rub over chicken thighs.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high. Sear chicken 3–4 minutes per side until browned (not cooked through). Remove.
  3. Lower heat to medium. Add onion, garlic, ginger; cook 2–3 minutes. Add diced tomatoes, cook 3–4 minutes.
  4. Add rinsed rice, garlic powder, turmeric, tomato bouillon, salt. Toast 1–2 minutes.
  5. Pour in your chosen liquid option. Stir well, add 3 whole thyme sprigs.
  6. Nestle chicken thighs on top of rice. Cover tightly. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 45 minutes until rice is tender and chicken cooked through.
  7. Remove thyme sprigs, fluff rice, adjust seasoning, serve.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Parboiled rice holds up best for baked rice dishes.
  • If skipping coconut, don't increase turmeric — it can overpower.
  • Uncover and broil 2–3 minutes at the end for color.
