Spicy Chicken Taco Sweet Potato

Per serving (2 servings total): ~330–350 cal, 35g protein, 23–25g carbs, 9–11g fat, ~3–4g fiber.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 1 medium sweet potato
  • 4 tbsp nonfat Greek yogurt, plain
  • 11 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs (2 big ones)
  • 4 tbsp cilantro lime sauce
  • 3 tbsp queso fresco
  • 2 tbsp pickled red onions (sugar free)
  • Sliced jalapeno

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Poke holes in potato, wrap in wet paper towel, microwave 8 minutes.
  2. Coat potato in avocado oil and seasoning, air fry at 400°F for 10–15 minutes.
  3. Coat chicken thighs in avocado oil and seasoning, air fry at 400°F for 20 minutes.
  4. Slice the chicken, load up the potato with yogurt, chicken, cilantro lime sauce, queso fresco, pickled onions, and jalapeno.

Health notes

Fits blood sugar/cholesterol goals well — high protein, moderate carbs, good fiber. Swap thighs for chicken breast for leaner option.
