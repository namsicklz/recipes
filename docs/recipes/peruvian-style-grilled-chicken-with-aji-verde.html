Peruvian-Style Grilled Chicken with Aji Verde

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 4–5 lbs bone-in chicken thighs
  • 3 tbsp aji amarillo sauce
  • 3 tbsp aji panca paste
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt & pepper, to taste

Aji Verde (Green Sauce)

  • 2 cups fresh cilantro, packed
  • 2–3 jalapeños, seeded for less heat
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • ½ cup mayonnaise
  • ¼ cup sour cream
  • Salt & pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Toss chicken thighs with aji amarillo, aji panca, lemon zest, lemon juice, paprika, cumin, oregano, garlic, salt, pepper. Mix well, cover, marinate at least 3 hours (overnight best).
  2. Preheat grill or oven to 375°F (190°C). If grilling, use indirect heat. Cook ~1 hour, or until internal temp reaches 165°F (74°C). Rest 10 minutes before serving.
  3. Blend cilantro, jalapeños, garlic, mayonnaise, sour cream, salt, pepper until smooth. Chill at least 30 minutes.
  4. Pile chicken on a platter, serve with chilled aji verde on the side.
