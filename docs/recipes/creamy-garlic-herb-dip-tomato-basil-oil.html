Creamy Garlic & Herb Dip + Tomato Basil Oil

Two dips, one roasted-garlic base.

Creamy Garlic & Herb Dip

  • 2 heads roasted garlic, mashed
  • 1 roasted sweet onion, mashed
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • ¼ cup parsley, minced
  • ¼ cup green onion, minced
  • ½ cup shredded Parmesan cheese
  • Salt & pepper, to taste
  • ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
  • Garnish: chopped parsley & a drizzle of olive oil

Instructions

  1. Mix roasted garlic and onion until smooth. Add softened cream cheese and sour cream; blend until creamy.
  2. Stir in parsley, green onion, Parmesan, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes.
  3. Chill 30 minutes before serving. Garnish with extra parsley and a light drizzle of olive oil.

Tomato Basil Oil

  • 1 head roasted garlic, mashed
  • 1 cup olive oil
  • 1–2 cups mashed tomatoes, salted
  • ¼ cup basil, minced
  • ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
  • ½ cup shredded Parmesan cheese
  • Drizzle of balsamic vinegar
  • Salt & pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Combine roasted garlic, olive oil, and mashed tomatoes in a bowl.
  2. Stir in basil, Parmesan, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper.
  3. Add a light drizzle of balsamic vinegar, mix well. Serve at room temperature with toasted bread, pasta, or as a veggie dip.

How to Roast Garlic & Onion

Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Slice tops off garlic heads and onion, place in foil or a small dish. Drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with salt. Cover tightly with foil, roast 1 hour until soft and golden. Cool, then squeeze garlic from skins.

📊 Nutrition (per 2-tbsp serving)

Creamy Garlic & Herb: 85 cal, 7g fat, 2g carbs, 2g protein, 110mg sodium.

Tomato Basil Oil: 90 cal, 8g fat, 3g carbs, 1g protein, 95mg sodium.
