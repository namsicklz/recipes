Juicing Recipes for Over 45

A set of cold-press juice blends (made for a Nama J2 juicer) built around: honeycrisp apples, oranges, kiwis, watermelon, lemon, lime, mango, strawberry, ginger, green grapes, and honeydew. Grouped by the health goal each one targets.

Green Glow Immunity Juice

Rich in vitamin C, antioxidants, and anti-inflammatory compounds.

  • 2 Honeycrisp apples (core removed)
  • 1 cup green grapes
  • 1-inch piece of ginger
  • ½ lime (peeled)
  • ½ lemon (peeled)

Feed apples first, then grapes, followed by citrus and ginger. Stir before serving.

Citrus Sunrise Digestive Boost

Supports gut health, boosts collagen production, and hydrates.

  • 2 oranges (peeled)
  • 1 kiwi (peeled)
  • ½ lemon (peeled)
  • ½ inch ginger
  • 1 cup strawberries (hulled)

Juice citrus and kiwi first, then strawberries and ginger. Best served cold.

Hydration Hero Juice

Excellent for hydration, skin elasticity, and blood pressure support.

  • 2 cups watermelon (rind removed)
  • ½ lime (peeled)
  • 1 kiwi
  • 1 cup strawberries
  • 1-inch piece of ginger (optional)

Juice watermelon first, followed by kiwi and berries. Add lime and ginger last.

Tropical Recovery Juice

Great for post-workout recovery and joint support (enzymes and antioxidants).

  • 1 mango (peeled and pitted)
  • 1 orange
  • 1 lemon (peeled)
  • 1 cup green grapes
  • ½ inch ginger

Juice grapes first, then mango, orange, lemon, finish with ginger. Stir well.

Heart-Healthy Red Juice

High in polyphenols and vitamin C — supports cardiovascular health.

  • 1 cup strawberries
  • 1 cup watermelon
  • 1 Honeycrisp apple
  • ½ lemon (peeled)

Juice apple and watermelon first, then strawberries and lemon.

Anti-Inflammation Elixir

Supports joint health, heart health, and immune resilience.

  • 2 Honeycrisp apples
  • 1 orange (peeled)
  • ½ lemon (peeled)
  • 1-inch piece of ginger
  • 1 kiwi (peeled)

Hydration & Skin Glow Juice

Supports skin elasticity, hydration, and kidney health.

  • 1 cup watermelon
  • 1 cup honeydew
  • ½ lime (peeled)
  • 1 cup green grapes
  • ½ orange

Liver & Gut Cleanser

Supports liver detox, digestion, and regularity.

  • 1 mango (peeled and pitted)
  • 1 kiwi
  • ½ lemon
  • ½ inch ginger
  • 1 cup green grapes

Cardio-Boost Juice

Supports blood pressure, cholesterol balance, and circulation.

  • 1 Honeycrisp apple
  • 1 cup strawberries
  • ½ orange
  • 1 cup watermelon
  • ½ lemon (peeled)

Morning Metabolism Starter

Supports energy, immunity, and blood sugar stability.

  • 2 oranges
  • 1 cup strawberries
  • ½ lime (peeled)
  • 1-inch piece of ginger
  • 1 kiwi

💡 Tips for Juicing Over 45

  • Alternate high-water fruits (melon, citrus) with denser produce (apples, mango) to improve juicing flow.
  • Include ginger frequently — reduces inflammation and supports digestion.
  • Drink juices slowly, ideally with some protein or fiber on the side to prevent blood sugar spikes.
  • Store extra juice in airtight glass containers, drink within 24–48 hours for best nutrient retention.
