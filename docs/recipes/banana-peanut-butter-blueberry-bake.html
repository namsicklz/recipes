Banana Peanut Butter Blueberry Bake

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 bananas
  • 250g peanut butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 150g blueberries

👩‍🍳 Instructions

Mash bananas, mix with peanut butter and eggs, fold in blueberries, then bake.

📝 Notes

Great protein content, kid-friendly. Can be adapted with chocolate chips or strawberries, made in a muffin tin, or air-fried.
