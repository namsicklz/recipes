Peri Peri Chicken

🧂 Ingredients (marinade, enough for ~1.5–2 kg chicken)

  • 3–4 red & green chillies
  • 4 bird's eye chillies (or peri peri chillies)
  • 1-inch piece fresh ginger
  • 6–8 garlic cloves
  • ½ medium onion
  • 1 whole bell pepper (any colour)
  • 3 bay leaves
  • Peel of ½ lemon
  • Juice of 4 lemons
  • 2 tbsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp dried thyme
  • 1 tbsp dried dill
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp vinegar
  • 1½ cups olive oil

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Blend all ingredients into a smooth marinade paste. Adjust seasoning to taste.
  2. Coat 1.5–2kg chicken (whole, thighs, or drumsticks) thoroughly. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours (overnight best).
  3. Grill/BBQ over medium-high heat until charred and cooked through, or roast at 200°C (400°F) for 35–40 minutes, turning once.
  4. Brush with extra marinade while cooking. Serve with fries, rice, or salad.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~150g chicken + marinade, ~6 servings)

~420 cal, 28g protein, 6g carbs, 31g fat.
