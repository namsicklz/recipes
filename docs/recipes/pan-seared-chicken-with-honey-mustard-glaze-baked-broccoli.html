Pan-Seared Chicken with Honey Mustard Glaze & Baked Broccoli

Serves: 2 · Prep: 10 min · Cook: 25 min

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (~6 oz each)
  • Kosher salt & freshly cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter

Honey-Mustard Glaze

  • 1½ tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp whole-grain mustard
  • 1½ tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar (or white wine vinegar)
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated or minced
  • Pinch of smoked paprika (optional)

Baked Broccoli

  • 2 cups fresh broccoli florets
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • Freshly cracked black pepper
  • Zest of ½ lemon
  • Optional: 1 tbsp grated Parmesan or pecorino

To Finish

  • Chopped fresh thyme or parsley
  • Lemon wedge

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toss broccoli florets with olive oil, salt, and pepper on a parchment-lined tray. Roast 18–20 minutes, flipping halfway, until edges are crisp and slightly charred. Toss with lemon zest and Parmesan (if using), set aside.
  3. Pat chicken dry, season both sides generously with salt and pepper, pound the thick end so each breast is even thickness.
  4. Heat olive oil in a stainless or cast-iron pan over medium-high heat. Sear chicken smooth-side down 3–4 minutes without moving. Flip, reduce to medium, add butter, and baste continuously (arroser) for 2–3 minutes. Internal temp should reach 160°F (71°C); it'll finish at 165°F as it rests. Remove to a plate.
  5. Whisk together the glaze ingredients. Pour off excess fat from the pan, keeping the browned bits. Add the glaze to the hot pan, simmer 30–60 seconds until glossy. Return chicken to the pan, turning to coat evenly, spooning glaze over several times so it forms a thin lacquer.
  6. Rest 2–3 minutes, slice on the bias, drizzle with remaining glaze, sprinkle herbs, finish with lemon.

Plating

Spoon a small pool of glaze on the plate, fan the sliced chicken over it, add roasted broccoli beside it, sprinkle herbs, finish with a micro-grate of lemon zest.

Chef's Tips

The charred broccoli cuts through the glaze's sweetness. A dusting of toasted sesame seeds adds crunch. Pairs well with a crisp Sauvignon Blanc or lightly oaked Chardonnay.
