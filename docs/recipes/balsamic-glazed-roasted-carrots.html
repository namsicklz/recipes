Balsamic Glazed Roasted Carrots

Source: recipeslady.com

🧂 Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 1 lb (450g) carrots, peeled, cut into sticks (or left whole if thin)
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbsp honey (or maple syrup for vegan option)
  • Optional: 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves, for garnish

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Toss carrots with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Arrange in a single layer.
  3. Roast 20–25 minutes, turning halfway, until tender and starting to caramelize.
  4. Whisk balsamic vinegar and honey together for the glaze.
  5. Remove carrots, drizzle with glaze, return to oven 5 more minutes until glazed.
  6. Garnish with thyme if desired, serve immediately.

📊 Nutrition (per serving)

~103 kcal, 4g fat, 18g carbs (4g fiber), 1g protein.
