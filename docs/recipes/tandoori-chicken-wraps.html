Tandoori Chicken Wraps (Chicken Tikka with Green Sauce)

Ingredients (serves 4–5)

Chicken Marinade

  • 750g boneless chicken (thighs preferred)
  • ½ tbsp salt
  • ½ tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • ½ tbsp cumin powder
  • ½ tbsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves
  • ½ tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger-garlic
  • 1 tbsp mint sauce
  • ¼ cup oil
  • ½ tsp yellow food colouring (optional)
  • ¼ cup yoghurt
  • Juice of 1 lemon

Green Sauce

  • ½ cup coriander
  • Handful fresh mint
  • 3–4 garlic cloves
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ⅓ cup yoghurt
  • ⅓ cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Juice of ½ lemon

Assembly

  • Soft naan or flatbread, chopped lettuce, sliced onions, chopped coriander

Instructions

  1. Combine all marinade ingredients, coat chicken, refrigerate at least 2 hours (overnight best).
  2. Bake, grill, or pan-fry at 200°C (400°F) for 20–25 minutes until cooked and slightly charred. Slice into strips.
  3. Blend green sauce ingredients until smooth.
  4. Warm naan, layer with lettuce, onions, chicken, drizzle with green sauce, sprinkle coriander.

Nutrition (per serving, ~5 servings)

~435 cal, 32g protein, 14g carbs, 27g fat.
