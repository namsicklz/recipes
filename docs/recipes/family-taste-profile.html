Family Taste Profile

One-time analysis (2026-11-05) of all ~130 recipes in this folder, built to give `/recipe-trend-scan` a real taste signal instead of just category-count gap math. Built from a keyword frequency pass across every recipe's ingredients/instructions plus the full title list — not vibes.

Flavor base: garlic + lemon, honey-glazed

  • Garlic appears in ~80/130 recipes (62%) and lemon in ~53/130 (41%) — this is the household's actual flavor foundation, not just "a" flavor. Often together: Garlic Lemon Butter Cod, Lemon Garlic Butter Salmon, Garlic & Rosemary Roast Potatoes, Chicken Kefta with Roasted Garlic Paprika Sauce.
  • Honey-glaze pattern in ~29/130, concentrated in chicken and sides: Garlic-Honey Glazed Chicken Thighs, Honey Chili Chicken, Garlic Sweet Chilli Baked Chicken, Honey Mustard Chicken, Avocado & Bacon Salad, Crispy Hot Honey Lemon Feta Chicken Cutlets, Crispy Smashed Sweet Potatoes with Whipped Feta & Hot Honey.

Warm spice over raw heat

  • Paprika (32), cumin (23), chili (22) show a Mediterranean/Middle Eastern/Cajun/Mexican-adjacent lean — Chicken Kefta, Classic Cajun Seasoning, Tandoori Chicken Wraps, Chicken Pulao Biryani, Mulligatawny Soup, Spicy Chicken Taco Sweet Potato.
  • Only ~18/130 touch any real heat (spicy/hot sauce/chili flake) and nothing in the library is genuinely hot — this family likes warm spice, not actual spiciness. A new recipe pitched as "fiery" or "intensely spicy" is a mismatch regardless of how trendy it is.

Secondary cuisine threads

  • Asian-inspired (soy/ginger/sesame, ~19/19/14 files): Black Pepper Beef Stir Fry, Crockpot Mongolian Beef & Noodles, Crockpot Shoyu Beef Yakisoba, 20-Minute Wonton Soup, Bang Bang Chicken, Hainanese-Inspired Chicken Rice, Ebi Katsu Sando.
  • Mediterranean (feta/tahini-chickpea/basil/balsamic/vinaigrette, roughly 5-10 files each): Mediterranean Chickpea Salad, Greek Tortellini Salad, Smashed Cucumber & Feta Salad, Mango Chickpea Salad, Grilled Zucchini & Chickpea Wraps.
  • Indian cuisine present but thin (3 dishes: Tandoori, Biryani, Mulligatawny) — a real but minor thread, not a gap to chase aggressively.

Texture and method

  • Crispy is a standing favorite (~22/130) across proteins and sides, not just one category: Crispy Hot Honey Lemon Feta Chicken Cutlets, Crispy Chicken Pasta Salad, Crispy Orange Chicken Tacos, Crispy Air Fryer Apple Fries, Crispy Roast Potatoes, Crispy Pork & Corn Dumplings.
  • Practical weeknight methods matter: slow cooker/crockpot (5 explicit, several more braised), sheet-pan one-pan (9), air fryer (5).
  • "Michelin-style" elevated home cooking is a real, named pattern (6 titles use the exact phrase: Michelin-Style Baked Spatchcock Chicken, Michelin Chicken Pesto Orzo, Michelin-Style Crushed Cucumber, Michelin-Style Pancakes) — means restaurant-plating of simple, accessible ingredients, not technically hard cooking. A good future-pitch angle.
  • Crowd-size batch cooking exists specifically for seafood (Lemon Garlic Butter Salmon (Crowd-Size), Garlic Lemon Butter Cod (Crowd-Size)) — feeding the full multi-household group at once.

Dessert profile

  • Caramel/brown-sugar sweetness (17 files) dominates over straight chocolate — Burnt Basque Cheesecake, Caramel Stuffed Brown Sugar Cookies, Salted Caramel Apple Campfire Pies, Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookie Fries.
  • Cheesecake is a recurring favorite format (4+ variations): 5-Layer Brownie Cookie Cheesecake, Burnt Basque Cheesecake, Rainbow Soufflé Cheesecake.

What's genuinely rare or absent (don't chase confidently)

  • Vegetarian/vegan (5/130) — matches the 1-recipe Vegetarian Mains category; a real, long-standing gap, not an oversight to second-guess.
  • Genuinely spicy/hot food — see above.
  • Scratch bread baking beyond pizza dough.
  • Dairy-free or other health-restriction cooking as a pattern.

Kid-friendly signal

Explicit kid-titled recipes (Kid-Friendly Steak Fried Rice, Kids Chocolate Chip Protein Balls) lean mild and familiar-format rather than adventurous. Since two of the four households include kids, any gap-fill suggestion for Breakfast or Vegetarian Mains should skew approachable over novel.

How to use this

`/recipe-trend-scan` should weigh a candidate's actual flavor/method fit against this profile, not just whether it fills an empty category slot. The 2026-11-05 test run's three rejected picks (spinach egg bites, lentil gyros, crockpot pork chops with mushroom gravy) were all gap-shaped but none hit the real signature here — garlic+lemon+honey base, warm (not hot) spice, crispy texture, or the Michelin-style elevated-simple angle. A gap-fill candidate that also matches one of those threads is a much stronger pitch than one that merely fills an empty slot.
