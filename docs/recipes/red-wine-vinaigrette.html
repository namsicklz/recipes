Red Wine Vinaigrette

🧂 Ingredients

  • ½ cup red wine vinegar
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp parsley
  • 2 tsp oregano
  • ¾ cup olive oil

👩‍🍳 Instructions

Whisk all ingredients together until emulsified.

Uses

Italian chopped salads, pasta salads, potato salads, grilled vegetables, chicken or steak salads. Keeps up to 1 week refrigerated — shake well before serving.
