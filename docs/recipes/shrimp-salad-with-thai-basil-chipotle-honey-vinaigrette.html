Shrimp Salad with Thai Basil, Cilantro & Chipotle-Honey Vinaigrette

Two versions: cooked shrimp, or raw-shrimp ceviche-style (acid-cured).

Salad (both versions, serves 3–4)

  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup cucumber, diced or thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup thinly sliced red onion
  • ½ cup Thai basil leaves, torn
  • ½ cup fresh cilantro leaves
  • Optional: avocado, mixed greens, or shredded romaine

Copycat Chipotle-Honey Vinaigrette

  • 1½–2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp adobo sauce (from canned chipotles) + 1 small chipotle pepper for more heat (optional)
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 2 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Blend everything except olive oil until smooth; drizzle in olive oil while blending to emulsify. Adjust: more honey for sweeter, more lime/vinegar for brighter, more adobo for smokier/hotter.

Version A — Cooked Shrimp

  1. Toss 1 lb large shrimp with 1 tbsp olive oil, ½ tsp kosher salt, ½ tsp smoked paprika, ¼ tsp cumin, ¼ tsp black pepper, juice of ½ lime.
  2. Sauté or grill 1½–2 minutes per side until just opaque. Cool slightly.
  3. Combine with salad ingredients, add shrimp, drizzle with vinaigrette, toss gently.

Version B — Raw Shrimp Ceviche-Style

Use sushi-grade or very fresh shrimp only.

  1. Finely chop 1 lb raw shrimp. Place in a glass/ceramic bowl with ½ cup fresh lime juice (~4 limes) and ¼ tsp kosher salt. Stir gently.
  2. Cover, refrigerate 15–20 minutes, stirring once, until opaque and firm (no translucent spots). Drain excess lime juice.
  3. Combine salad ingredients, fold in cured shrimp, drizzle vinaigrette lightly (don't overdress). Rest 5 minutes, serve.

💡 Chef Notes

Thai basil is essential — don't substitute Italian basil. Soak raw onion in ice water 5 minutes if it feels too sharp. Best eaten same day; excellent chilled or room temp.
