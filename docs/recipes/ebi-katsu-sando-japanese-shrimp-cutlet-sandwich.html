Ebi Katsu Sando (Japanese Shrimp Cutlet Sandwich)

Source: feedthepudge.com. Makes 4 servings. Prep 30 min, cook 15 min. Crispy panko-coated shrimp cutlets in soft shokupan (Japanese milk bread) with tangy tartar sauce and cabbage.

🧂 Ingredients

Ebi Katsu

  • 2 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 egg white
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp sugar
  • ½ tsp white pepper

Coating

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cups panko breadcrumbs
  • Oil for frying

Japanese Tartar Sauce

  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp pickles, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Kewpie mayo
  • 1 small shallot, diced
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Accompaniments

  • Butter
  • Shredded cabbage
  • Shokupan (Japanese milk bread)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Finely chop two-thirds of the shrimp into a paste; roughly chop the remaining third for texture.
  2. Combine shrimp with egg white, cornstarch, salt, sugar, and white pepper. Mix vigorously 1–2 minutes until sticky and cohesive.
  3. Oil your hands and press the mixture into patties slightly larger than the bread slices. Freeze on parchment for 30 minutes.
  4. Hard-boil eggs for 10 minutes, cool, peel, and chop. Mix with pickles, mayo, shallot, and lemon juice. Season generously; refrigerate.
  5. Set up three bowls: flour, beaten eggs, and panko.
  6. Dredge each patty in flour, shake off excess, dip in egg, then press firmly into panko on both sides.
  7. Heat oil to 350°F. Fry patties 4–5 minutes per side until golden brown. Transfer to a wire rack.
  8. Butter the bread slices. Layer katsu, cabbage, and tartar sauce between slices. Press gently and slice into thirds. Serve immediately.

📝 Notes

"Ebi" means shrimp, "katsu" means panko-breaded deep-fried cutlet, "sando" is just Japanese for sandwich — a variation of the broader katsu-sando family (usually pork or chicken), adapted here with shrimp.
