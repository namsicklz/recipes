Crockpot Shoyu Beef Yakisoba

Slow cooker chuck roast in a shoyu-brown sugar sauce, tossed with yakisoba noodles at the end. Source: 406_bbq.

Prep 15 minCook 4–5 hrs (LOW) or 2½–3½ hrs (HIGH)Serves 4–6

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 lbs thinly sliced chuck roast
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • ½ cup low-sodium shoyu
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup low-sodium beef broth
  • 2 Tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 Tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 Tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 thinly sliced onion
  • 2 Tbsp cornstarch + 2 Tbsp cold water
  • 12 oz precooked noodles (Fortune Yakisoba)
  • Green onions, sesame seeds & chili crisp, to finish
  • Your favorite beef seasoning (recipe used Holy Gospel by Meat Church)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Season beef and sear in butter over high heat, 1–2 minutes per side, in batches. Just build color.
  2. Add sliced onion and beef to the crockpot. Deglaze the skillet with a splash of beef broth and pour into the crockpot.
  3. Add remaining broth, shoyu, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, vinegar and pepper. Stir.
  4. Cook on LOW 4–5 hours or HIGH 2½–3½ hours. Check early for tenderness.
  5. Remove 1 cup of sauce and bring to a boil in a saucepan. Mix cornstarch with cold water, whisk into the boiling sauce until thickened, then return to the crockpot.
  6. Cook on HIGH 10–15 minutes until sauce is thick and glossy.
  7. Loosen noodles under hot water. Add to crockpot with 1-inch green onion pieces. Toss and heat 5–10 minutes.
  8. Finish with sesame seeds, green onions and chili crisp for heat.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Serve immediately — don't leave the noodles on KEEP WARM or they'll soak up the sauce.
