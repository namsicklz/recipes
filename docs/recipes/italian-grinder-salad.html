Italian Grinder Salad

Source: savoringitaly.com

🧂 Ingredients (serves 4)

Dressing:

  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise (olive oil variety recommended)
  • 1/3 cup plain non-fat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 2 tbsp pepperoncini juice
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmigiano Reggiano cheese
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper

Salad:

  • 1 head iceberg lettuce, chopped
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup chopped pepperoncini or banana peppers
  • 1 cup cucumbers, diced
  • 1/4 cup Genoa salami, chopped (~8 slices)
  • 1/4 cup deli turkey, chopped (~8 slices)
  • 1/4 cup deli ham, chopped (~8 slices)
  • 4 slices provolone cheese, chopped

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Whisk together mayonnaise, Greek yogurt, red wine vinegar, pepperoncini juice, Parmigiano Reggiano, garlic powder, oregano, salt, and pepper until smooth. Adjust seasoning to taste.
  2. Chop the lettuce, salami, turkey, ham, and provolone. Thinly slice the red onion, halve the cherry tomatoes, dice the cucumbers, and chop the pepperoncini.
  3. Combine lettuce, red onion, pepperoncini, tomatoes, salami, turkey, ham, and provolone in a large mixing bowl.
  4. Pour dressing over the salad right before serving and toss thoroughly to coat. Serve immediately for best crunch.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~343 cal, 17g protein, 9g carbs, 27g fat.
