Oven-Baked Chicken Shawarma Loaf

Juicy, flavorful chicken marinated in aromatics and spices, then baked into a loaf for easy slicing. Great for meal prep, bowls, wraps, or salads.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs (trimmed of excess fat if desired)
  • 1 medium yellow onion, processed or finely grated
  • Juice of 1 large lemon
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp plain or Greek yogurt
  • 3–4 pinches kosher salt
  • Freshly cracked black pepper, to taste
  • 1½ tbsp dried thyme
  • 1½ tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp urfa pepper (substitute Aleppo pepper or red chili flakes)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Marinate: In a large bowl, combine chicken thighs, processed onion, lemon juice, tomato paste, yogurt, salt, pepper, thyme, garlic powder, and urfa pepper. Mix well until chicken is fully coated. Cover and refrigerate 2–6 hours (overnight for best flavor).
  2. Prep: Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Let chicken sit at room temperature 30 minutes. Pack marinated chicken tightly into a loaf pan (8.5x4.5x2.5" works well).
  3. Bake: Bake uncovered for 50 minutes. Carefully tilt the pan to drain excess juices into a jar, then pour juices back over the chicken once or twice to baste. Save remaining juices for soups or sauces.
  4. Slice & serve: Flip loaf onto a cutting board and slice thinly into strips. Serve in a bowl, wrap, pita, or over rice.

🍽️ Serving Ideas

  • Low-carb bowl: cauliflower rice, fresh veggies, tahini sauce
  • Classic wrap: warm pita, lettuce, tomato, cucumber, garlic sauce
  • Meal prep: quinoa or low-carb rice for balanced lunches

📊 Nutrition

Whole loaf: ~2,436 cal, 258g protein, 27g carbs, 138g fat.

Per serving (6 servings): ~406 cal, 43g protein, 4.5g carbs, 23g fat.
