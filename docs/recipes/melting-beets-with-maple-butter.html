Melting Beets with Maple Butter

A sweet-savory roasted beet side dish with a maple-sherry butter glaze.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2½ lbs medium red beets (~5), trimmed, peeled & sliced into 1-inch rounds (~6 cups)
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • ⅓ cup low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
  • ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 500°F. Toss beet slices with olive oil, salt, pepper. Arrange in a single layer in a 9x13-inch metal baking pan (do NOT use glass at 500°F).
  2. Roast 35–40 minutes, flipping halfway, until edges brown and centers are tender.
  3. Whisk together vegetable broth, maple syrup, melted butter, sherry vinegar, and Dijon mustard. Pour carefully over roasted beets.
  4. Return to oven uncovered, roast another 8 minutes, until glaze thickens and beets are very tender.
  5. Transfer to a serving platter, spoon over remaining pan juices, sprinkle with fresh thyme.
