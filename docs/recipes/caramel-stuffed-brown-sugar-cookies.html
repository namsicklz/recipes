Caramel Stuffed Brown Sugar Cookies

Source: Carlsbad Cravings. Soft, chewy brown-sugar cookies with a gooey caramel center and a hint of sea salt. No chilling time needed.

Makes: ~20–24 cookies · Total time: ~38 min (25 min prep + ~13 min baking)

Ingredients

  • 14 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 cups + 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ tsp baking powder
  • 1¾ cups packed brown sugar (light, medium, or dark)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 large egg + 1 large egg yolk
  • 1 tbsp vanilla extract
  • 20–24 soft caramel candies (e.g. unwrapped caramel squares)
  • Sea salt, for sprinkling on top

Instructions

  1. Brown the butter: Melt 10 tbsp butter in a skillet until golden brown (1–3 min), then mix in the remaining butter pieces and let cool.
  2. Whisk flour, baking soda, and baking powder together.
  3. Stir cooled brown butter with brown sugar and salt. Add egg, yolk, and vanilla. Mix in dry ingredients just until combined.
  4. Stuff with caramel: Scoop ~1 tbsp of dough, flatten, place an unwrapped caramel in the center, top with another tablespoon of dough, pinch and roll into a ball.
  5. Place on a prepared baking sheet ~3 inches apart, sprinkle with sea salt. Bake at 350°F for ~11–13 minutes until barely golden.
  6. Cool slightly — cookies firm up a bit but stay soft and chewy with a melty caramel core.

Why it works

Brown butter, brown sugar, and an extra egg yolk give depth of flavor and chewiness; the caramel center plus sea salt makes for a decadent contrast.
