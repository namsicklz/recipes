Mayak Gyeran (Korean Soy-Marinated Jammy Eggs)

"Drug eggs" — jammy eggs cured in a savory-sweet soy marinade.

Yield: 4 eggs · Marinate: 12–24 hours

Ingredients

Eggs

  • 4 large eggs

Marinade

  • ⅓ cup soy sauce
  • ⅓ cup water
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru (to taste)
  • 3–4 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 1 green chili, finely chopped
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • 2–3 tbsp finely chopped white onion
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil. Gently lower eggs in, cook exactly 7 minutes. Transfer immediately to an ice bath 5 minutes. Peel carefully (slightly older eggs peel easier).
  2. Combine soy sauce, water, gochugaru, garlic, chili, onions, vinegar, honey, sesame seeds in a bowl. Taste and adjust: more honey for sweeter, more soy for saltier, more water for milder.
  3. Place peeled eggs in a shallow container, pour marinade over, ensure fully submerged. Cover, refrigerate 12–24 hours, turning once if not fully covered.

Serving

Over warm rice (classic), avocado toast, ramen, salads, or sliced in grain bowls.

Storage

Keeps 3–4 days refrigerated; flavor peaks around 18 hours. Leftover marinade works for tofu, chicken, stir-fries, or noodle sauce.

Healthier version

Low-sodium soy sauce, reduce honey to ½ tbsp, extra scallions + sesame, serve with brown rice or cucumbers instead of white rice.

Pro upgrade

Add ½ tsp grated ginger, ½ tsp sesame oil, squeeze of lime before serving.
