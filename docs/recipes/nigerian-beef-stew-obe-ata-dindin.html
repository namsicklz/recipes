Nigerian Beef Stew (Obe Ata Dindin)

Bold, spicy, deeply savory pepper-tomato stew with slow-simmered beef.

🧂 Ingredients (serves 6–8)

Beef

  • 2 lbs (900g) beef (stew beef, short ribs, or chuck), cut into chunks
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 2 seasoning cubes (Maggi or Knorr) or 2 tsp bouillon powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup water

Stew Base (Pepper Mix)

  • 6 medium red bell peppers
  • 3 medium tomatoes
  • 2 Scotch bonnet peppers (adjust to heat preference)
  • 1 large onion

Cooking the Stew

  • ½–¾ cup vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1–2 seasoning cubes
  • Salt & pepper to taste

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Combine beef, onion, garlic, thyme, curry powder, seasoning cubes, salt in a pot. Add water, cover, simmer 30–40 minutes until tender. Reserve stock.
  2. Blend bell peppers, tomatoes, Scotch bonnets, and onion into a smooth puree.
  3. Heat oil in a large pot. Add tomato paste, fry 3–4 minutes. Add garlic and ginger, stir briefly.
  4. Pour in pepper puree. Cook on medium-high 15–20 minutes, stirring often, until oil floats on top and raw taste cooks off.
  5. Add curry, thyme, seasoning cubes, salt, and beef stock. Stir well. Add cooked beef, simmer 15 more minutes until thick and flavorful.
  6. Serve with rice, fried plantains, yams, or bread.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~8 servings)

~420 cal, 28g protein, 10g carbs, 30g fat.
