Chicken Pulao Biryani

Source: @shadi_faleel — a simplified 30-minute-style biryani.

🧂 Ingredients (serves 6–8)

Chicken Masala

  • 1 kg chicken (bone-in)
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 cardamoms, 4 cloves
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • ½ tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp cumin powder
  • ½ tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp coriander powder
  • ½ tbsp turmeric powder
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, finely chopped
  • ½ cup oil
  • ½ cup yoghurt
  • Juice of 1 lemon

Rice

  • 700g basmati rice
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • Juice of ½ lemon (add squeezed lemon half to boiling rice)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Handful fresh coriander

Finishing

  • 4–5 tbsp rose water
  • ½ tsp saffron colouring
  • Handful fresh coriander

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Heat oil, add cumin seeds, cinnamon, bay leaves, cardamom, cloves; sauté until fragrant. Add onion, fry golden brown.
  2. Add tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, spices, salt; cook until oil separates. Stir in chicken, yoghurt, lemon juice; cook until chicken is tender and coated.
  3. Wash and soak basmati rice. Boil in salted water with lemon juice, squeezed lemon half, cumin seeds, and oil until 80% cooked. Drain.
  4. Layer chicken masala at the bottom of a large pot, rice on top. Sprinkle rose water, saffron colouring, coriander.
  5. Cover tightly and cook on low heat (dum) for 15–20 minutes until rice is fluffy.
  6. Serve hot with raita or salad.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~8 servings)

~545 cal, 34g protein, 55g carbs, 20g fat.
