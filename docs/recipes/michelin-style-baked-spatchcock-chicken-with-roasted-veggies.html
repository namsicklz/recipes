Michelin-Style Baked Spatchcock Chicken with Roasted Veggies

Chicken roasts on a rack directly above a vegetable tray so the fat and butter baste the veggies below as it cooks.

🧂 Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1 whole chicken (~3½–4 lb), spatchcocked (backbone removed, flattened)
  • 3 tbsp softened unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh rosemary, finely chopped (plus 2 sprigs for the pan)
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • Juice of ½ lemon

Vegetable tray

  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 3–4 large carrots, peeled, cut into thick batons
  • 8 oz cremini or button mushrooms, halved
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt + pepper to taste
  • Optional: 1 tsp balsamic vinegar for glaze at the end

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large roasting tray with parchment or foil.
  2. Toss potatoes, carrots, mushrooms, and onions in olive oil, salt, and pepper. Scatter rosemary sprigs among them.
  3. Mix butter, olive oil, garlic, chopped rosemary, smoked paprika, lemon zest, salt, and pepper into a paste. Loosen chicken skin over the breast and thighs, spread half the mixture underneath, rub the rest over the exterior. Finish with a squeeze of lemon juice.
  4. Place the veggie tray on the bottom. Set a wire rack directly above the vegetables, lay the spatchcocked chicken on the rack, skin side up — the drippings fall onto the vegetables as it cooks.
  5. Roast 50–60 minutes, until chicken's internal temp hits 165°F (74°C) at the thickest part of the breast. Toss the veggies gently halfway through to re-coat in drippings.
  6. Rest the chicken 10–15 minutes before carving. Optionally drizzle veggies with balsamic vinegar and pan juices before serving.

💡 Chef's Touch

Sprinkle fleur de sel and fresh rosemary leaves over the carved chicken. For a pan sauce: whisk a tablespoon of Dijon mustard into the roasting pan drippings with a splash of white wine, reduce briefly.
