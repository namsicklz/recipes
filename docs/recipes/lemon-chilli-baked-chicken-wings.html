Lemon & Chilli Baked Chicken Wings

Source: @shadi_faleel — zero-oil baked wings.

🧂 Ingredients (serves ~4)

  • 600g chicken wings
  • ¾ tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • ⅓ cup lemon juice
  • ½ cup plain flour
  • 1 tsp lemon zest (in coating)
  • 1 tbsp lemon zest (for garnish)
  • 1 tbsp dried chives or parsley (or fresh)
  • ½ cup sweet chilli sauce

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Wash and pat dry wings. Mix garlic powder, onion powder, salt, black pepper, cumin, lemon juice, flour, and 1 tsp lemon zest into a coating. Coat wings evenly.
  2. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking tray with parchment. Bake 30 minutes, flipping at the 15-minute mark.
  3. Toss baked wings in sweet chilli sauce. Garnish with extra lemon zest and parsley/chives.
  4. Serve hot with a dipping sauce of choice.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~320 cal, 28g protein, 14g carbs, 16g fat.
