Potato Meat Pie (Fata'ir Batata bil Lahm al-Mafaroom)

Mashed-potato dough pies stuffed with spiced minced meat and mozzarella.

🧂 Ingredients

Dough

  • 2 large potatoes (about 450g)
  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup fine semolina
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tbsp mixed spices, made from: 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, ½ tsp smoked or sweet paprika, ½ tsp ground turmeric, ½ tsp ground cinnamon, ½ tsp black pepper, ¼ tsp ground cloves (optional), ¼ tsp chili powder or cayenne (optional)
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp melted butter

Meat Filling

  • ½ kg (500g) minced beef or lamb
  • 1 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt & black pepper, to taste
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese

👩‍🍳 Instructions

Dough

  1. Peel and boil potatoes until fork-tender (~20 min). Drain and mash in a large bowl.
  2. Add flour, semolina, eggs, salt, mixed spices, baking powder, and melted butter. Mix until a soft dough forms — if sticky, add more flour 1 tbsp at a time.
  3. Cover and let rest 15–20 minutes while preparing the filling.

Filling

  1. Heat butter and oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add onion, cook until translucent.
  2. Add minced meat, cook until browned, breaking apart with a spoon.
  3. Stir in Worcestershire sauce, paprika, salt, pepper. Cook 3–5 minutes more.
  4. Remove from heat, cool slightly, then mix in shredded mozzarella.

Assemble & Bake

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C), line a baking tray with parchment.
  2. Divide dough into golf-ball-sized portions. Flatten each into a disc, add 1–2 tbsp filling to the center, fold into a half-moon or round pie. Seal edges with a fork or by pinching.
  3. Brush tops with melted butter or egg wash.
  4. Bake 20–25 minutes until golden brown and crisp. Let cool slightly before serving.

🍽️ Serving Tip

Serve warm with yogurt mixed with mint and lemon, or a small side salad.
