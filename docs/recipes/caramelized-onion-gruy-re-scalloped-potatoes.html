Caramelized Onion & Gruyère Scalloped Potatoes

Rustic, cheesy, rich — no mandolin needed.

Ingredients

Caramelized Onions

  • 5 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar

Potatoes

  • 6 Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and sliced into thick rounds

Cheese Sauce

  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 cups shredded Gruyère cheese
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack or white cheddar
  • 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 1 tbsp chopped thyme
  • Salt & pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Caramelize onions: Heat olive oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add onions, season with salt and pepper, cook until deeply golden (25–35 min). Add red wine vinegar, cook until absorbed. Remove and set aside.
  2. Keep sliced potatoes submerged in water until ready to use (prevents browning).
  3. Cheese sauce: Melt butter in the same pot over medium heat. Add flour, cook 1 minute, whisking. Slowly whisk in milk and cream. Simmer, whisking, until thickened. Stir in Gruyère, Monterey Jack/cheddar, rosemary, thyme, salt, pepper.
  4. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C). Spread a thin layer of cheese sauce in a baking dish. Layer potatoes, season lightly, add more sauce, then a layer of caramelized onions. Repeat, finishing with cheese sauce and extra cheese on top.
  5. Cover with foil, bake 1 hour. Remove foil, bake 30 minutes more until bubbling, golden, and potatoes are tender.
  6. Rest 10 minutes before serving for clean slices. Finish with extra thyme or chives if desired.
