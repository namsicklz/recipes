Honey Mustard Chicken, Avocado & Bacon Salad

🧂 Ingredients (serves 4)

Chicken

  • 2 large chicken breasts (~500g total)
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Honey Mustard Dressing

  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar (or lemon juice)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Salad Bowl

  • 6 cups mixed greens (romaine, spinach, arugula, etc.)
  • 1 large avocado, sliced
  • 6 slices cooked crispy bacon, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ red onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ cucumber, sliced

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Season chicken with paprika, garlic powder, salt, pepper, olive oil. Grill or pan-sear over medium heat 6–7 minutes per side (internal temp 165°F/74°C). Rest 5 minutes, slice.
  2. Whisk Dijon mustard, honey, olive oil, vinegar/lemon juice, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  3. Arrange greens, avocado, bacon, tomatoes, onion, and cucumber in a large bowl. Top with sliced chicken.
  4. Drizzle with honey mustard dressing, toss lightly before serving.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, ~4 servings)

~485 cal, 33g protein, 14g carbs, 33g fat.
