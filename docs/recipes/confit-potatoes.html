Confit Potatoes

Restaurant-quality potatoes — crispy outside, buttery inside. Two methods below (traditional pressed, and a faster no-press version), plus a bacon variation.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 3 lbs Yukon Gold potatoes (or small russets), peeled, cut into thick rounds or wedges
  • 2–2½ cups duck fat, beef tallow, or neutral oil
  • 6 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 4–6 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 2 sprigs rosemary (optional)
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 1–2 tsp kosher salt
  • Optional: 1 bay leaf

To finish: flaky sea salt, fresh thyme or chives, optional Parmesan or garlic aioli.

Bacon variation: add 6–8 strips thick-cut bacon, chopped.

👩‍🍳 Method A — Traditional (pressed, best texture)

  1. Peel and slice/wedge potatoes, rinse briefly, pat dry.
  2. Place in a heavy pot with fat, garlic, herbs, peppercorns, salt. Heat gently over medium-low, then reduce to low and cook 45–60 minutes until completely tender but not falling apart — creamy and soft when pierced.
  3. Line a loaf pan with parchment, transfer warm potatoes in even layers, pour a little confit fat over. Fold parchment over the top, weight it down with another pan, and refrigerate at least 2 hours (overnight best) — this compression makes them sliceable and ultra-crispy when fried.
  4. Cut into cubes, rectangles, or fries. Fry in a thin layer of duck fat or oil over medium-high heat until deep golden and crisp on all sides.
  5. Season with flaky salt, herbs, Parmesan, or aioli.

👩‍🍳 Method B — No-Press, Same Day

  1. Same prep and slow-confit step as above (45–60 minutes, gentle poach, don't let it boil).
  2. Drain potatoes with a slotted spoon, remove herbs and garlic.
  3. Crisp using one of: pan-fry (2–3 tbsp confit fat, sear until deep golden, flip, repeat), air fryer (lightly coat in fat, 400°F for 10–14 minutes, shaking halfway), or oven (450°F for 15–20 minutes, flipping once).
  4. Season and serve — inside melting/buttery, outside crisp and golden, no pressing or chilling needed.

Bacon Variation

  1. Cook chopped bacon in a cold skillet, heat to medium, cook until crispy. Remove bacon, reserve all the fat.
  2. Add the bacon fat to the confit fat along with the potatoes and aromatics; proceed with Method B's slow-confit and crisping steps.
  3. Once potatoes are crispy, sprinkle with crispy bacon pieces, fresh thyme/chives, flaky salt, pepper. Optional: garlic aioli or Parmesan.

💡 Chef Tips

Duck fat gives the best flavor. Don't skip the low, slow cook. Can be made 3 days ahead and crisped to order. Flavor upgrades: ½ tsp smoked paprika when crisping, sauté onions in bacon fat, or toss with ranch seasoning after crisping.
