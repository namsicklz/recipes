Crockpot Mongolian Beef & Noodles

Source: social recipe share (Crockpot Series Episode 7). Tender chuck roast in a sweet and savory Mongolian-style sauce, tossed with noodles.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 pounds thinly sliced chuck roast
  • 4 tablespoons butter, for searing
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium shoyu
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup low-sodium beef broth
  • 2 tablespoons minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons cold water
  • 12 ounces precooked lo mein noodles
  • sliced green onions
  • sesame seeds, optional
  • chili crisp oil, optional

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Season the beef with SPG or your favorite seasoning. Heat a skillet over high heat with the butter and sear the beef in small batches, 1-2 minutes per side, to build color (not cook through).
  2. Transfer the beef to the crockpot. Deglaze the skillet with a splash of the measured beef broth, scraping up the browned bits, and pour into the crockpot.
  3. Add the remaining broth, shoyu, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and black pepper. Stir gently.
  4. Cover and cook on LOW 4-5 hours or HIGH 2.5-3.5 hours. Check early so the thin beef stays tender without falling apart.
  5. Mix the cornstarch and cold water into a slurry, stir into the sauce, and cook on HIGH 15-20 minutes until thick and glossy.
  6. Slice green onions into 1-inch pieces. Separate the precooked noodles and add directly to the crockpot. Toss everything together and heat on HIGH 5-10 minutes.
  7. Finish with sliced green onions, sesame seeds, and chili crisp oil if desired. Serve immediately — don't leave the noodles on KEEP WARM, they'll keep soaking up the sauce.
