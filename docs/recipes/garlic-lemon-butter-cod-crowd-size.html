Garlic Lemon Butter Cod (Crowd-Size)

A large single piece of white fish instead of individual fillets — mild, affordable, holds together well when baked, and easy to buy as one large center-cut side or loin.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 4.5–5 lbs cod (ask for a "center-cut side" or large loin piece)
  • 6 tbsp butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 lemon (juice + slices)
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • ⅓ cup panko breadcrumbs
  • Fresh parsley
  • Salt + pepper
  • Optional: paprika, grated parmesan, capers

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 400°F.
  2. Put cod in a lightly oiled baking dish or sheet pan.
  3. Mix melted butter, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Pour over the fish.
  4. Sprinkle panko on top, add lemon slices.
  5. Bake about 18–25 minutes depending on thickness.
  6. Finish with parsley and a squeeze of lemon.

📝 Notes

The panko top gets slightly crispy while the fish stays flaky and buttery. Halibut is a pricier, more "upscale" alternative if budget allows, but gets expensive fast for a crowd.

Serve With

Spanish roasted potatoes, roasted asparagus or green beans, simple arugula salad, warm bread, white wine or sangria.
