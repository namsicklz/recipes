Sweet Potato Crème Brûlée

Reconstructed recipe — the original was only ever saved as a generated PDF that's no longer recoverable. This is a plausible version based on the name. Makes 6 servings.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 1½ cups roasted sweet potato puree (about 1 large sweet potato, roasted and mashed)
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cinnamon stick (or ½ tsp ground cinnamon)
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 5 egg yolks
  • ½ cup granulated sugar, plus more for the brûlée topping
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C). Place six ramekins in a deep baking dish.
  2. Heat the cream with the cinnamon stick and nutmeg in a saucepan until just simmering. Remove from heat, cover, and steep 10 minutes. Discard the cinnamon stick.
  3. In a bowl, whisk the egg yolks, sugar, and salt until pale. Slowly whisk in the warm cream, then stir in the sweet potato puree and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Divide the mixture among the ramekins. Pour hot water into the baking dish until it reaches halfway up the sides of the ramekins.
  5. Bake 35–40 minutes, until the custards are set around the edges but still slightly jiggly in the center.
  6. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate at least 2 hours (or overnight).
  7. Just before serving, sprinkle a thin, even layer of sugar over each custard and caramelize with a kitchen torch (or under a broiler) until deep golden and crackly.

📝 Notes

Let the brûléed sugar sit 1–2 minutes before serving so it hardens into a proper crackable shell.
