Michelin-Style Crushed Cucumber in Oil & Vinegar

Bright, crisp, aromatic, silky — a refined, restaurant-grade take on smashed cucumber.

Serves 2–3Time 15 min (plus 10 min resting)

🧂 Ingredients

Cucumbers

  • 2 English cucumbers or 4 Persian cucumbers
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp sugar

Dressing

  • 2 tbsp Champagne vinegar (or white wine vinegar)
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp high-quality extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1½ tsp soy sauce
  • 1 small garlic clove, shaved or grated
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (small amount — perfume only)
  • ½ tsp chili flakes or 1 fresh red chili, finely sliced
  • 1 tsp honey (or a pinch)
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Finishing

  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Small handful fresh herbs (dill, cilantro, mint, or Thai basil — pick 1–2)
  • Zest of ½ lemon
  • Flaky salt

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Cut cucumbers in half lengthwise, smash lightly cut-side down with the flat side of a knife until they crack. Tear into irregular bite-size pieces. Toss with salt and sugar, let sit 10 minutes, then drain and pat dry.
  2. Whisk together all dressing ingredients — it should taste bright, acidic, slightly sweet, savory, and aromatic on its own.
  3. Toss cucumbers with dressing. Add sesame seeds, fresh herbs, lemon zest. Finish with a pinch of flaky salt. Chill 5–10 minutes before serving.

Plating

Chilled shallow bowl, loose mound of cucumbers in the center, dressing spooned around the edges (not over the top), finished with a drizzle of olive oil, fresh herbs, sesame seeds, and a whisper of lemon zest.
