Chicken Sausage & Kale Rice Soup

🧂 Ingredients

  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp butter (or more)
  • Splash of olive oil
  • 4 carrots, chopped
  • 4 celery stalks, chopped
  • 2 cups uncooked white rice, rinsed
  • 6 cups chicken bone broth
  • 1 pack pre-cooked chicken sausage, sliced
  • 1½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp sea salt (or more to taste)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 3 large handfuls kale, chopped
  • Handful fresh parsley, chopped

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Melt butter in a large pot over medium heat. Cook onion and garlic until fragrant but not browned.
  2. Add carrots and celery, cook 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in rice, turmeric, black pepper, sea salt, and garlic powder.
  4. Pour in chicken bone broth and sliced sausage, bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce to a simmer, cook ~10–12 minutes.
  6. Add kale, cover, simmer another 5–10 minutes until rice is tender and kale is wilted.
  7. Top with fresh parsley, serve warm.

📊 Nutrition (per serving, with full macro version, 6 servings)

~448 cal, 16g protein, 60g carbs, 16g fat.
