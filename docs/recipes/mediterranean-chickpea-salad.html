Mediterranean Chickpea Salad

Source: recipegirl.com

Ingredients (serves 8)

  • 2 (15-oz) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 large cucumber, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ¼ cup red onion, diced
  • 4 oz (⅓ cup) crumbled feta cheese
  • ¼ cup finely chopped parsley

Lemon Vinaigrette

  • 4 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced
  • ¼ tsp ground cumin
  • Salt & pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Toss chickpeas, vegetables, feta, and parsley together in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk (or shake) together all dressing ingredients.
  3. Drizzle dressing over the salad, toss until well coated.
  4. Chill at least 30 minutes before serving — marination enhances flavor.

Nutrition (per serving, 8 servings)

300 cal, 12g protein, 34g carbs, 14g fat.
