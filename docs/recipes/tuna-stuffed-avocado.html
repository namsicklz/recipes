Tuna Stuffed Avocado

Source: The Stay At Home Chef. A bright, healthy twist on tuna salad — no mayo, served in creamy avocado halves.

🧂 Ingredients (serves 2)

  • 1 avocado, halved and pitted
  • 1 (5 oz) can solid white albacore tuna, drained
  • ¼ cup diced red bell pepper
  • 1 tbsp minced jalapeño
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Scoop a bit of each avocado half to enlarge the bowl area; mash that with tuna, bell pepper, jalapeño, cilantro, and lime juice in a bowl.
  2. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  3. Spoon the mixture into the avocado halves and enjoy immediately.

📊 Nutrition (per avocado half)

225 cal, 14g protein, 10g carbs, 15g fat, 7g fiber (2g sat fat, 166mg sodium, 659mg potassium).
