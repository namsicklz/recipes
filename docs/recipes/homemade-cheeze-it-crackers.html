Homemade Cheeze-It Crackers

Reconstructed recipe — the original was only ever saved as a generated PDF that's no longer recoverable. This is a plausible version based on the name, not the original. Makes about 6 dozen small crackers.

🧂 Ingredients

  • 8 oz sharp cheddar, finely shredded (freshly grated melts better than pre-shredded)
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, cold, cubed
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp salt, plus flaky salt for topping
  • ¼ tsp paprika (optional, for color)
  • 2–3 tbsp ice water

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. In a food processor, pulse the cheddar and butter together until combined.
  2. Add flour, salt, and paprika; pulse until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs.
  3. Add ice water 1 tablespoon at a time, pulsing until the dough just comes together.
  4. Shape into a disc, wrap, and chill 30 minutes.
  5. Roll the dough out thin (about ⅛ inch) between two sheets of parchment. Cut into 1-inch squares.
  6. Transfer to a parchment-lined baking sheet, poke a small hole in the center of each with a toothpick, and sprinkle with flaky salt.
  7. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–15 minutes, until golden and crisp. Cool completely on the pan — they crisp further as they cool.

📝 Notes

Store in an airtight container; they stay crisp for about a week at room temperature.
