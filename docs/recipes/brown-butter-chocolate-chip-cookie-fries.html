Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookie Fries with Chocolate & White Chocolate Dips

Source: cookwithshaaa

🧂 Ingredients

  • ¾ cup (168g) unsalted butter
  • 1 cup (200g) packed light brown sugar
  • ¼ cup (50g) granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg + 1 large egg yolk, at room temperature
  • 1 tbsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1¾ cups (220g) all-purpose flour
  • ¾ tsp baking soda
  • ¾ tsp kosher salt + more flaky sea salt for sprinkling
  • 1½ cups (255g) semi-sweet chocolate chips or finely chopped chocolate

Plus: melted chocolate and white chocolate for dipping.

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Brown the butter: Melt in a small saucepan over medium-low heat, stirring and scraping every 10–15 seconds. It will foam, then bubbles shrink and it turns golden brown with a nutty aroma (5–7 min). Remove from heat, cool a few minutes.
  2. Combine brown butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar in a large bowl, mix until smooth. Add egg, extra yolk, and vanilla; mix well.
  3. Add flour, baking soda, and salt. Gently fold until dough is soft but not sticky — don't overmix. Fold in chocolate chips. Cover, refrigerate at least 1 hour (up to overnight).
  4. Let dough soften at room temperature ~20 minutes until scoopable. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C).
  5. On a parchment-lined surface, roll or press dough into a rectangle about ½ inch thick (1–1.5cm). Cut into "fries" about 3–4 inches long (8–10cm) and ½ inch wide (1.5cm). Arrange on a parchment-lined baking sheet with space between pieces.
  6. Bake 12–13 minutes until edges are light golden brown, centers still soft. Cookies will spread — quickly reshape into straight fries with a flat spatula right after removing from oven if needed.
  7. Cool completely before moving (fragile while warm). Sprinkle with flaky salt, serve with melted chocolate and white chocolate dips.
