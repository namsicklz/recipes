Mango Chickpea Salad

Source: Vegan Richa. Naturally cholesterol-free, high fiber and plant protein.

Ingredients

Salad

  • 1½ cups cooked chickpeas (or 1 can, drained)
  • 1 ripe mango, diced
  • ¼–½ small red onion, finely chopped
  • Handful fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Optional: cucumber or bell pepper for crunch

Cumin-Lime Toasted Pepper Dressing

  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1–2 tsp olive oil (optional)
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • Fresh cracked black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional: pinch chili flakes for heat

Instructions

  1. Whisk lime juice, cumin, black pepper, salt, oil, and chili flakes for the dressing.
  2. Combine chickpeas, mango, onion, cilantro, and any extra veggies.
  3. Add dressing, mix well. Let sit 5–10 minutes so flavors absorb.
  4. Serve chilled or room temp — as a light lunch, taco/wrap filling, or side with grilled protein.
