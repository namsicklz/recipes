Smoked Salmon Cucumber Sushi Boats

Quick sushi fix without the roll — packed with omega-3s.

Ingredients

  • 1 large cucumber
  • ½ cup cooked sushi rice
  • 8 oz smoked salmon
  • 1½ tbsp teriyaki sauce or soy glaze
  • ½ tsp sesame oil (optional)
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 green onion, finely sliced
  • Soy sauce or spicy mayo, for dipping

Instructions

  1. Slice cucumber in half lengthwise. Scoop out seeds with a spoon to create shallow boats, pat dry.
  2. Spoon sushi rice into each cucumber half, pressing gently to form an even base.
  3. Layer smoked salmon evenly over the rice.
  4. Top with teriyaki sauce, sesame oil (if using), sesame seeds, and green onions.
  5. Slice into bite-sized pieces or enjoy whole. Serve with soy sauce or spicy mayo.
